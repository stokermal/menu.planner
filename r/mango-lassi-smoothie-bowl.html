Mango Lassi Smoothie Bowl with Toasted Coconut Muesli

Love Real Food, p. 2

30 min · 7 1/2 cups muesli and four 1-cup servings of mango yogurt servings · 355 cal · 20g protein

Ingredients

  • Muesli: 4 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
  • Muesli: 1 1/2 cups unsweetened shredded coconut
  • Muesli: 3/4 cup raw pepitas (hulled pumpkin seeds)
  • Muesli: 3/4 cup sliced almonds
  • Muesli: 3/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • Muesli: 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • Muesli: 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
  • Muesli: 3 tablespoons maple syrup or honey
  • Muesli: 2 tablespoons melted coconut oil
  • Muesli: 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • Muesli: 1/2 cup chia seeds
  • Mango yogurt: heaping 2 cups frozen mango chunks
  • Mango yogurt: 2 cups plain Greek yogurt
  • Optional sweeteners: pitted Medjool dates, honey, or maple syrup

Steps

  1. To make the muesli: preheat the oven to 350 degrees F. Line a large rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. In a large bowl, combine the oats, coconut, pepitas, almonds, salt, cinnamon, and ginger. Stir. Pour in the maple syrup, coconut oil, and vanilla. The chia seeds go in after baking. Stir until well blended.
  3. Pour onto the baking sheet and smooth into an even layer. Bake until the oats and coconut are lightly golden and fragrant, about 15 minutes, tossing halfway. Set aside to cool.
  4. Once cooled to room temperature, stir in the chia seeds. Store in a freezer bag with the air squeezed out. It keeps best in the freezer, no thawing needed, up to 3 months.
  5. To make the mango yogurt: blend the mango and yogurt until smooth. If the frozen mango is too hard, let it thaw 5 to 10 minutes and try again. Taste and add a sweetener if needed.
  6. Spoon the mango yogurt into bowls and top with muesli. Serve immediately. Refrigerate remaining yogurt up to 5 days.
