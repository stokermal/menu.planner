Seasoned Taco Ground Beef

House recipe

20 min · about 8 tacos worth servings · 210 cal · 24g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds 90/10 or 93/7 ground beef
  • 1 packet (or 2 tablespoons) taco seasoning
  • 2/3 cup water

Steps

  1. Brown the ground beef in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up as it cooks, until no pink remains.
  2. Drain if needed. At 90/10 or 93/7 there is usually little to pour off, which is the point of buying the leaner grind.
  3. Stir in the taco seasoning and water. Simmer 3 to 5 minutes until the liquid thickens and coats the meat.
  4. Taste and adjust. Keep warm until the table is ready.
