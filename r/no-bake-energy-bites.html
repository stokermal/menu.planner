No-Bake Energy Bites

Gimme Some Oven (gimmesomeoven.com), by Ali Martin

20 min · 20-25 energy bites servings · 105 cal · 3g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 cup old-fashioned oats
  • 2/3 cup toasted shredded coconut (sweetened or unsweetened)
  • 1/2 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 1/2 cup ground flaxseed
  • 1/2 cup semisweet chocolate chips
  • 1/3 cup honey
  • 1 tablespoon chia seeds (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Stir all of the ingredients together in a large mixing bowl until thoroughly combined.
  2. Cover the bowl and chill in the refrigerator 1-2 hours, until the mixture is cold. This makes it hold together when rolled.
  3. Roll the mixture into 1-inch balls.
  4. Serve right away, or refrigerate in a sealed container up to 1 week, or freeze up to 3 months.
