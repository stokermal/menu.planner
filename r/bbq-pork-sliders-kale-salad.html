BBQ Pork Sliders with Sweet Kale Salad

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 47

20 min · Serves 6-8 servings · 470 cal · 24g protein

Note: BBQ sauce: used Kinder's & Sweet Baby Ray's. Used dif. rolls.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups carnitas (about 1/2 of a bag)
  • 1/2 cup BBQ sauce
  • 1 (14 oz) bag sweet kale salad kit
  • 1 (16-ct) package Hawaiian rolls

Steps

  1. Heat the carnitas according to the package directions.
  2. Add the BBQ sauce to the meat and stir well.
  3. Meanwhile, in a large bowl, assemble the sweet kale salad kit.
  4. Slice the entire package of Hawaiian rolls in half and open like one big sandwich. Place the two open faces on a sheet pan and broil until toasted.
  5. Add the BBQ pork and salad (using it like a slaw) to the toasted Hawaiian rolls.
  6. Serve with leftover salad on the side.
