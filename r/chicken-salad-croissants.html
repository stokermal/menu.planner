Chicken Salad Croissants

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 89

15 min · Serves 6-8 servings · 540 cal · 30g protein

Note: Doubled & used whole chicken but it was too much sauce! Still Yummy!

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1 Tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1/2 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp seasoned salt
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1/2 rotisserie chicken, chopped (about 4 cups)
  • 2 stalks celery, finely chopped
  • 2 cups grapes, halved
  • 6-8 croissants

Steps

  1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the mayonnaise, sour cream, Dijon, lemon juice, sugar, seasoned salt, salt, and pepper. Stir until smooth.
  2. Add the chicken, celery, and grapes and mix until combined.
  3. Cut the croissants in half and spoon in the chicken salad.
