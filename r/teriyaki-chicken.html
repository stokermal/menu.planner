Teriyaki Chicken

House recipe

30 min · about 6 servings · 330 cal · 38g protein

Note: House staple. The same sauce also goes on frozen meatballs.

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken breasts, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 tablespoon neutral oil
  • 1 recipe Teriyaki Sauce (p. 46), about 1 1/4 cups
  • Optional: sesame seeds and sliced green onions to finish
  • Rice, to serve

Steps

  1. Make the Teriyaki Sauce if it is not already in the fridge. It keeps well, so a double batch is worth it.
  2. Cut the chicken into 1-inch pieces so everything finishes at the same time.
  3. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add the chicken in a single layer and leave it alone for 3 to 4 minutes to brown before stirring. Crowding or stirring too early makes it steam and go grey.
  4. Cook another 4 to 5 minutes, until the pieces reach 165 degrees F.
  5. Lower the heat, pour in the teriyaki sauce, and toss 2 to 3 minutes until it thickens and coats. The sauce already has cornstarch in it, so it will glaze quickly — pull it before it goes gluey.
  6. Finish with sesame seeds and green onions and serve over rice.
