Burst Cherry Tomato, Basil, and Goat Cheese Scrambled Egg Toasts

Love Real Food, p. 41

25 min · 4 servings servings · 350 cal · 18g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 pint cherry or grape tomatoes
  • 1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt, divided
  • 4 large slices (1/2-inch thick) crusty whole-grain bread
  • 4 ounces goat cheese, at room temperature
  • 6 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons neutral-flavored milk of choice
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • A couple pats of unsalted butter or 2 teaspoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh basil, divided

Steps

  1. In a large heavy-bottomed stainless steel saucepan, warm the olive oil over medium until shimmering. Add the tomatoes and 1/4 teaspoon of the salt and cover the pot. Cook, shimmying the pot occasionally, until most of the tomatoes burst out of their skins and the oil is reddish and saucy, 5 to 7 minutes. Remove from heat and set aside.
  2. Meanwhile, toast the bread until lightly golden and crisp. Spread each warm slice generously with goat cheese and set aside.
  3. Crack the eggs into a medium bowl. Add the milk, the remaining 1/4 teaspoon salt, and a few twists of pepper. Beat with a whisk until thoroughly combined and pure yellow.
  4. In a medium skillet, melt the butter over medium-high, swirling to coat. Pour in the egg mixture, then use a heat-resistant spatula to scrape the eggs along the bottom and sides until they begin to clump and the spatula leaves a trail, about 1 1/2 minutes.
  5. Reduce heat to low and gently fold the eggs over on themselves until clumpy but still slightly wet, 30 to 60 seconds. Off the heat, stir in half the tomatoes and half the basil. Season to taste.
  6. Divide the scrambled eggs on top of the prepared toasts. Top with the remaining tomatoes and basil. Serve immediately.
