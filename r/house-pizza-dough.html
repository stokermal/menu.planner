The Best Pizza Dough (House Recipe)

Sugar Spun Run (sugarspunrun.com), by Sam Merritt

60 min · 12 servings; makes one 10-12 inch pizza servings · 113 cal · 3g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 to 2 1/3 cups all-purpose flour OR bread flour
  • 1 packet instant yeast (2 1/4 teaspoons)
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons sugar
  • 3/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/8 to 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder and/or dried basil
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, plus additional
  • 3/4 cup warm water (175 ml)

Steps

  1. Combine 1 cup (125g) of flour, instant yeast, sugar, and salt in a large bowl. Add the garlic powder and dried basil here too.
  2. Add olive oil and warm water and stir very well with a wooden spoon.
  3. Gradually add another 1 cup (125g) of flour, plus any additional flour as needed (sometimes up to another 1/3 cup), stirring until the dough forms a cohesive, elastic ball and starts pulling away from the sides of the bowl. It will still be slightly sticky but manageable.
  4. Drizzle a separate large clean bowl generously with olive oil and brush up the sides.
  5. With lightly floured hands, form the dough into a ball and transfer to the oiled bowl. Roll it along the inside until coated, cover tightly with plastic wrap, and set somewhere warm.
  6. Let rise 30 minutes or until doubled. Preheat the oven to 425 F now so it is truly hot when the pizza is ready.
  7. Gently deflate the risen dough, move to a lightly floured surface, and knead briefly until smooth, about 3 to 5 turns.
  8. Work the dough into a 12 inch circle with your hands or a rolling pin.
  9. Transfer to a parchment-lined pizza pan and pinch or fold the edges to form a crust.
  10. Drizzle about a tablespoon of olive oil over the top and brush the whole surface, crust included.
  11. Poke holes all over the center with a fork so it does not bubble.
  12. Add toppings and bake at 425 F for 13 to 15 minutes, until golden. Heavy toppings may need longer.
