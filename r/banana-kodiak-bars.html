Banana Kodiak Bars

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 23

40 min · Serves 10-12 servings · 325 cal · 6g protein

Ingredients

  • 6 overripe bananas
  • 1 cup brown sugar, packed
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup salted butter, melted
  • 3 cups Kodiak Cakes Flapjack and Waffle Mix
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • for serving: butter and honey

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Spray a 9x13" baking dish with nonstick spray.
  2. In a large bowl, mash the bananas. Add the brown sugar, eggs, and butter. Mix well.
  3. Add the Kodiak Cakes mix, baking soda, and salt. Mix until combined.
  4. Pour the mixture into the baking dish.
  5. Bake for 25-30 minutes, until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  6. Serve with butter and honey, if desired.
