Crispy Corn Tortilla Strips

Our Best Bites, p. 141

25 min · Up to 12 tortillas' worth servings · 155 cal · 2g protein

Ingredients

  • Canola oil, enough to fill a large skillet 1/2 to 1 inch deep
  • Up to 12 corn tortillas
  • Kosher salt, to taste

Steps

  1. Heat about 1/2 to 1 inch of oil in a large skillet.
  2. While the oil is heating, use a pizza wheel to cut the tortillas into thin strips.
  3. Test the oil's temperature by tossing a small corner of corn tortilla into the oil. If it sizzles violently, turn the heat down. If the strip sinks, rises to the top, and bubbles, the oil is ready.
  4. Cook all the tortilla strips, in batches if necessary, and drain on a paper towel. Sprinkle with kosher salt and serve as desired.
