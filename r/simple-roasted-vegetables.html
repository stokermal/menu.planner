Simple Roasted Vegetables

House recipe

35 min · about 6 as a side servings · 110 cal · 4g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 large head broccoli (about 1 pound), cut into florets
  • 1 small head cauliflower (about 1 pound), cut into florets
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425 degrees F.
  2. Cut the broccoli and cauliflower into florets of roughly the same size. They are the same density and roast at the same rate, which is why this pair needs no staging.
  3. Toss with the olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  4. Spread on a sheet pan in a single layer with space between the pieces. Crowding is what makes roasted vegetables steam instead of brown — use two pans rather than piling one.
  5. Roast 22 to 25 minutes, stirring once, until the edges are browned and the stems give easily to a knife.
  6. Taste and add more salt if needed. Serve hot.
