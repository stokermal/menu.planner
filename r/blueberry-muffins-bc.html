Blueberry Muffins

Betty Crocker Cookbook, p. 67

40 min · 12 muffins servings · 185 cal · 3g protein

Note: Doubled quantities written in the margin: 1 1/2 cups milk, 1/2 cup oil, 2 eggs, 4 cups flour, 1 cup sugar, 4 tsp baking powder, 2 cups blueberries. Page is pink-tabbed and heavily used.

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup fresh, canned (drained), or frozen blueberries

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 400 degrees F. Grease the bottoms only of 12 medium muffin cups with shortening, spray with cooking spray, or line with paper baking cups.
  2. In a large bowl, beat the milk, oil, and egg with a fork or wire whisk until well mixed. Stir in the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt all at once, just until the flour is moistened. The batter will be lumpy. Fold in the blueberries. Divide the batter evenly among the muffin cups.
  3. Bake 20 to 25 minutes or until golden brown. If baked in a greased pan, let stand about 5 minutes in the pan, then remove to a wire rack. If baked in paper cups, remove from the pan to a wire rack immediately. Serve warm if desired.
