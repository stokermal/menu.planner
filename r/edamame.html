Edamame

House recipe

7 min · about 6 as a side servings · 130 cal · 11g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 pound frozen edamame, in the pod or shelled
  • Flaky or kosher salt
  • Optional: toasted sesame oil, sesame seeds, or a squeeze of lime

Steps

  1. Boil or steam the frozen edamame 4 to 5 minutes, until bright green and tender. They are already blanched before freezing, so this is really just heating them through — going longer turns them dull and mushy.
  2. Drain well and toss with a generous pinch of flaky salt while still hot, so it sticks.
  3. Finish with sesame oil, sesame seeds, or lime if using. Serve warm or cold.
  4. In-pod edamame is eaten by pulling the beans through your teeth and discarding the pod — put out a bowl for the empties. Shelled is easier for a plate.
