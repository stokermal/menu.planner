Pesto BLT's

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 61

15 min · Serves 6 servings · 470 cal · 19g protein

Ingredients

  • 6 artisan rolls
  • 1/3 cup pesto
  • 1/3 cup mayonnaise
  • 18 slices fully cooked bacon
  • 2-3 tomatoes, sliced
  • 2 cups spring salad mix

Steps

  1. Split the rolls in half and broil face up for 1-2 minutes, until lightly toasted.
  2. In a small bowl, mix the pesto and mayonnaise.
  3. Add the bacon to a paper towel-lined plate and microwave for 1-2 minutes, until crispy.
  4. Spread the pesto sauce on both sides of the toasted rolls. Layer with the bacon, sliced tomatoes, and spring salad mix.
