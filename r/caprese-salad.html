Caprese Salad with Balsamic

House recipe

10 min · about 6 as a side servings · 215 cal · 12g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 pounds ripe tomatoes, sliced 1/4-inch thick (or 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved)
  • 12 ounces fresh mozzarella, sliced (or 2 cups mozzarella balls)
  • 1 cup fresh basil leaves
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar, or balsamic glaze
  • 1/2 teaspoon flaky or kosher salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

Steps

  1. Slice the tomatoes and mozzarella about the same thickness and shingle them on a platter, alternating and tucking basil leaves between.
  2. Salt the tomatoes a few minutes before serving, not hours before. Salt pulls water out, which is good for flavor and bad for a watery platter if you do it too early.
  3. Drizzle with the olive oil, then the balsamic. Adding the oil first keeps the balsamic from beading straight off the mozzarella.
  4. Finish with black pepper and serve at room temperature. Cold mutes tomatoes — pull them out of the fridge 20 minutes ahead if you can.
