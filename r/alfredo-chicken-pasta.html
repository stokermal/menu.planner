Alfredo Chicken Pasta

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 87

30 min · Serves 6-8 servings · 560 cal · 25g protein

Ingredients

  • 6 breaded chicken tenderloins
  • 1 lb spaghetti
  • 1 (24.5 oz) jar alfredo sauce
  • for garnish: shredded Parmesan cheese and fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Bake the chicken according to the package directions.
  2. Meanwhile, bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the spaghetti until al dente.
  3. Drain the noodles and place them back in the pot, then add the alfredo sauce. Stir the noodles and alfredo sauce until the sauce is heated through.
  4. Slice the chicken and serve over the alfredo pasta.
  5. Garnish with Parmesan and parsley, if desired.
