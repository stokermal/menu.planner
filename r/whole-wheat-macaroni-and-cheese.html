Whole Wheat Macaroni and Cheese

100 Days of Real Food (100daysofrealfood.com), by Lisa Leake

20 min · 4 people servings · 336 cal · 15g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 cup whole-wheat macaroni, boiled according to package directions
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons whole-wheat flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 cup grated cheese (sharp white cheddar, or a mix of cheeses)
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • Grated Parmesan cheese, optional topping

Steps

  1. Boil the macaroni according to the package directions and drain.
  2. Melt the butter in a saute pan over medium heat.
  3. Whisk the flour into the melted butter and keep whisking 1-2 minutes, until the roux darkens slightly but does not burn.
  4. Lower the heat and quickly whisk in the milk. Bring the heat back to medium and keep whisking until the mixture thickens and no lumps of flour remain.
  5. Stir in the grated cheese. Once it melts, fold in the cooked noodles and season with salt and pepper. Serve right away.
