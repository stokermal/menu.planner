Gingersnaps

Betty Crocker Cookbook, p. 180

35 min · about 4 dozen cookies servings · 65 cal · 1g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 3/4 cup shortening
  • 1/4 cup molasses
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cloves
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • Granulated sugar, for dipping

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 375 degrees F. Lightly grease a cookie sheet with shortening or spray with cooking spray.
  2. In a large bowl, beat the brown sugar, shortening, molasses, and egg with an electric mixer on medium speed, or mix with a spoon. Stir in the remaining ingredients except the granulated sugar.
  3. Shape the dough by rounded teaspoonfuls into balls. Dip the tops into granulated sugar. On the cookie sheet, place the balls sugared sides up, about 3 inches apart.
  4. Bake 10 to 12 minutes or just until set. Immediately remove from the cookie sheet to a wire rack.
