Loaded Nachos

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 133

20 min · Serves 6 servings · 560 cal · 23g protein

Note: YUM!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups carnitas (about 1/2 of a bag)
  • 1 (11 oz) bag tortilla chips
  • 1/2 can black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 2 cups shredded Mexican cheese
  • for topping: tomatoes, white queso, cilantro lime crema, chopped green onions, and avocado/guacamole

Steps

  1. Heat the carnitas according to the package directions.
  2. Add the tortilla chips in a single layer to a sheet pan.
  3. Top with the carnitas and beans, then sprinkle with the shredded cheese.
  4. Broil until the cheese is melted.
  5. Pull out of the oven and add desired toppings.
  6. Enjoy right from the pan!
