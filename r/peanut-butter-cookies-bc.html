Peanut Butter Cookies

Betty Crocker Cookbook, p. 180

30 min · about 2 1/2 dozen cookies servings · 115 cal · 2g protein

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • 1/4 cup shortening
  • 1/4 cup butter or margarine, softened
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • Additional granulated sugar, for flattening

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 375 degrees F.
  2. In a large bowl, beat 1/2 cup granulated sugar, the brown sugar, peanut butter, shortening, butter, and egg with an electric mixer on medium speed, or mix with a spoon. Stir in the flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Shape the dough into 1 1/4-inch balls. On an ungreased cookie sheet, place the balls about 3 inches apart. Flatten in a crisscross pattern with a fork dipped in additional granulated sugar.
  4. Bake 9 to 10 minutes or until light brown. Cool 5 minutes, then remove from the cookie sheet to a wire rack.
