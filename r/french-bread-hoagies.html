French Bread, Hoagies, and Sandwich Rolls

Our Best Bites, p. 32

40 min · Makes 1 loaf of French bread or 8-10 hoagies or sandwich rolls servings · 240 cal · 7g protein

Note: French bread. YUM!

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon active dry or bread machine yeast
  • 1 1/2 cups warm water
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 egg, separated
  • 4-5 1/2 cups flour, divided
  • 2 tablespoons cold water

Steps

  1. Combine the yeast, water, and sugar in a large mixing bowl and allow to stand 10 minutes or until bubbly.
  2. Add salt, vegetable oil, and egg yolk (set the white aside for later) and mix well until combined. Add enough remaining flour to make a soft dough that barely sticks to your finger. Knead, either by hand or in a mixer, for another 2-3 minutes. Cover and allow to rise 1 hour and 45 minutes (1 1/2 hours will do in a pinch).
  3. Punch down the dough. Spray a cookie sheet with nonstick cooking spray. For a loaf of bread, shape into a loaf and place on the sheet. For buns or hoagies, divide equally into 8-10 (or even 12) pieces and shape as desired. Use a scale, if you have one, to get equal-sized buns.
  4. It's okay if dough pieces touch (or will touch after having risen). Cover and allow to rise another 1 1/4-1 1/2 hours or until doubled.
  5. Preheat oven to 375 degrees F. Mix the egg white with the cold water and brush over the dough. Bake for 10 minutes and repeat brushing the dough with the egg wash.
  6. Bake an additional 8-10 minutes or until the bread is golden brown and your house smells like heaven.
