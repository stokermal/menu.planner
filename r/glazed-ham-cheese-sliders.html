Glazed Ham and Cheese Sliders

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 123

30 min · Serves 6 servings · 580 cal · 28g protein

Note: YUM!

Ingredients

  • 1 (16-ct) package Hawaiian rolls
  • 14 slices black forest ham (about 1 lb)
  • 9 slices Havarti cheese (about 1/2 lb)
  • 4 Tbsp salted butter
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp Italian Spice & Salt (or 1/2 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Slice the entire package of Hawaiian rolls in half and open like one big sandwich. Place the bottom half on a lined sheet pan and layer with folded ham and sliced cheese. Put the top half back on.
  3. In a small bowl, melt the butter and stir in the mustard and Italian Spice & Salt. Pour the mixture over the rolls and brush to coat evenly.
  4. Bake for 20 minutes.
  5. Slice the giant sandwich into individual sliders and serve.
