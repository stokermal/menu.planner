Roasted Carrots

House recipe

40 min · about 6 as a side servings · 110 cal · 1g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds carrots, peeled and cut into 1/2-inch diagonal slices
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • Optional to finish: chopped parsley, a squeeze of lemon, or a drizzle of honey

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425 degrees F.
  2. Cut the carrots on a diagonal into 1/2-inch slices. The angle gives more cut surface, and more cut surface means more browning.
  3. Toss with the olive oil, salt, and pepper, and spread on a sheet pan in a single layer with space between the pieces.
  4. Roast 28 to 32 minutes, stirring once, until the edges are deeply browned and the centers are tender. Carrots are dense, so they take longer than most vegetables and reward the extra time — the sugars caramelize and they come out sweet.
  5. Finish with parsley, lemon, or honey if using.
