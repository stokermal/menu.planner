Fresh Taco Salad with Creamy Avocado-Lime Dressing

Love Real Food, p. 56

45 min · 4 large salads servings · 435 cal · 11g protein

Ingredients

  • Taco filling: 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • Taco filling: 4 cloves garlic, pressed or minced
  • Taco filling: 1 1/2 teaspoons chili powder
  • Taco filling: 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • Taco filling: 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • Taco filling: 1/2 cup quinoa, rinsed
  • Taco filling: 1 cup water
  • Taco filling: 1 can (15 ounces) black beans, rinsed and drained, or 1 1/2 cups cooked black beans
  • Taco filling: 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • Taco filling: freshly ground black pepper
  • Crispy tortilla strips: 1 1/2 teaspoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • Crispy tortilla strips: 3 corn tortillas, cut into 2-inch-long, 1/4-inch-wide strips
  • Crispy tortilla strips: fine sea salt
  • Dressing: 1/4 cup lime juice (from about 2 limes)
  • Dressing: 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • Dressing: 1 tablespoon water
  • Dressing: 1/4 cup lightly packed fresh cilantro (some stems are okay)
  • Dressing: 1 small-medium jalapeno, seeded, deribbed, and roughly chopped
  • Dressing: 1 clove garlic, roughly chopped
  • Dressing: 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • Salad: 1 small head romaine lettuce, chopped (or 5 ounces chopped romaine)
  • Salad: 3 cups packed baby arugula (about 3 ounces)
  • Salad: 1 cup thinly sliced grape or cherry tomatoes
  • Salad: 1/3 cup thinly sliced and roughly chopped radishes (3 to 4), or chopped red onion
  • Salad: 1/3 cup crumbled feta cheese (about 2 ounces)
  • Salad: 1/2 medium-large avocado

Steps

  1. To make the taco filling: in a medium saucepan, warm the olive oil over medium until shimmering. Add the garlic, chili powder, and cumin. Cook until fragrant, stirring constantly, about 30 seconds. Add the tomato paste and saute another minute, stirring constantly. Add the rinsed quinoa and water and stir. Bring to a gentle boil, then cover and reduce heat to a gentle simmer. Cook until the liquid is absorbed, 15 to 20 minutes.
  2. Remove from heat and let rest, covered, 5 minutes. Uncover and fluff the quinoa with a fork. Gently stir in the black beans and salt. Season to taste. Cover and set aside a couple minutes to warm the beans.
  3. To make the tortilla strips: in a large skillet, warm the olive oil over medium until shimmering. Toss in the tortilla strips, sprinkle with salt, and stir. Cook until crispy and turning golden, stirring occasionally, 5 to 10 minutes. Drain on a paper-towel-lined plate.
  4. To make the dressing: in a small food processor or blender, combine the lime juice, olive oil, and water. Scoop in the half avocado and add the cilantro, jalapeno, garlic, and salt. Process until smooth. The dressing should be zippy, but if it is unpleasantly tart or hard to blend, dilute with 1 to 2 tablespoons of water and blend again.
  5. To assemble the salads: combine the romaine and arugula in a large bowl. Wait to dress the salad until you are ready to serve, since the greens wilt fairly quickly once dressed. If you are not eating all 4 servings right away, portion off the greens you want to save and dress only what you will eat now.
  6. Drizzle the greens with just enough dressing to lightly coat them; you might have extra. Toss until the dressing is evenly distributed.
  7. Arrange the dressed greens in 4 individual salad bowls, or dinner plates if your bowls are small. Top each salad with taco filling, tomatoes, radishes, crispy tortilla strips, and feta. Slice the remaining avocado half into long strips and place a couple on each salad. Serve immediately.
  8. If you have leftover dressing, press plastic wrap against the surface to prevent browning and refrigerate. Leftover tortilla strips get tough within a couple of hours, so you may want to skip those and add crushed tortilla chips to leftovers instead.
