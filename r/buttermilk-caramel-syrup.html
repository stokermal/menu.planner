Buttermilk Caramel Syrup

Our Best Bites, p. 70

11 min · Makes about 2 cups servings · 110 cal · 0g protein

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup buttermilk
  • 1 1/2 cups sugar
  • 1/2 cup real butter (no substitutions)
  • 2 tablespoons corn syrup
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla

Steps

  1. Combine buttermilk, sugar, butter, corn syrup, and baking soda in a 4-quart pan.
  2. Bring mixture to a boil and reduce heat to low—just make sure it's still bubbling. Cook, stirring very frequently, for 7-9 minutes or until the syrup reaches a rich golden brown. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla.
