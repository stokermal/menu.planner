Kodiak Power Cakes

House recipe

20 min · about 18 four-inch flapjacks, 6 servings servings · 310 cal · 21g protein

Note: Always made with whole milk and eggs, never water.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups Kodiak Power Cakes buttermilk mix
  • 2 1/4 cups whole milk
  • 3 large eggs
  • Butter or oil for the griddle
  • To serve: syrup, fruit, or nut butter

Steps

  1. Whisk the mix, whole milk, and eggs together until just combined. Do not overbeat — a few lumps are correct, and beating it smooth makes the flapjacks tough.
  2. Milk and eggs instead of water is the whole point: Kodiak lists 15g protein made with water, 18g with milk, and 21g with milk and an egg. Same mix, half again as much protein.
  3. Let the batter sit 2 to 3 minutes while the griddle heats. Use it within 15 minutes.
  4. Heat a griddle or skillet to 375 degrees F, or medium. It is ready when a drop of water skitters across the surface instead of sitting still.
  5. Pour 1/4 cup batter per flapjack. Cook until bubbles form and the edges look set, about 2 to 3 minutes, then flip once and cook until golden.
  6. Serve hot. Flip only once — pressing them down with the spatula squeezes out the air that makes them light.
