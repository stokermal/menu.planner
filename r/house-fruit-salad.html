House Fruit Salad

House recipe

15 min · about 8 as a side servings · 110 cal · 1g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 cups strawberries, hulled and quartered
  • 1 cup blueberries
  • 1 cup raspberries or blackberries
  • 3 cups cubed melon (cantaloupe, honeydew, or watermelon)
  • 3 kiwi, peeled and sliced
  • 2 apples, cored and diced
  • 2 bananas, sliced
  • Juice of 1 lime or 1/2 lemon
  • 1 tablespoon honey (optional)

Steps

  1. Cut the sturdy fruit first — melon, apples, kiwi, strawberries — and combine in a large bowl.
  2. Toss with the lime or lemon juice right away. The acid keeps the apples and bananas from browning, and it makes everything taste more like itself.
  3. Stir in the honey if using. It is optional and depends entirely on how sweet the fruit already is — taste first.
  4. Add the raspberries or blackberries and the bananas last, and fold gently. Both break down fast, and bananas turn the whole bowl soft if they go in early.
  5. Serve cold. If it is sitting more than an hour, hold the bananas back and add them at the table.
