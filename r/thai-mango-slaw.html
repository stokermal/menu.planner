Thai-Style Mango Slaw

Love Real Food, p. 55

75 min · 4 large or 8 side salads servings · 310 cal · 9g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 cup wild rice, rinsed
  • Slaw: 4 cups thinly sliced red cabbage (about 1 pound), roughly chopped into 2-inch-long strips
  • Slaw: 2 ripe mangoes, diced
  • Slaw: 1 red bell pepper, chopped
  • Slaw: 1/2 cup chopped green onions (about 4)
  • Slaw: 1/2 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • Dressing: 1/4 cup lime juice (from about 2 limes)
  • Dressing: 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • Dressing: 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
  • Dressing: 2 cloves garlic, pressed or minced
  • Dressing: 1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • Dressing: 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add the rinsed rice and continue boiling, reducing heat as necessary to prevent overflow, until the rice is pleasantly tender but still offers a light resistance, 40 to 55 minutes. Drain and return to the pot. Cover and let rest 10 minutes, then uncover and set aside.
  2. Meanwhile, to prepare the slaw: in a large serving bowl, combine the cabbage, mangoes, bell pepper, green onions, and cilantro.
  3. To prepare the dressing: in a small bowl, whisk all the dressing ingredients until blended.
  4. Add the warm rice to the slaw and toss to combine. Drizzle in the dressing and toss again. Season to taste with additional salt.
  5. Serve immediately, or chill until ready to serve. Keeps well chilled 3 to 4 days. Wake up the flavors with an extra squeeze of lime.
