Baked French Toast with Maple Cream Syrup

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 17

65 min · Serves 6-8 servings · 730 cal · 15g protein

Ingredients

  • FRENCH TOAST:
  • 8 eggs
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 (1 lb) loaf French bread, cubed
  • MAPLE CREAM SYRUP:
  • 1 cup real maple syrup
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 2 Tbsp salted butter
  • TOPPING:
  • 1 Tbsp brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • for serving: powdered sugar

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, cream, vanilla, and 2 tsp cinnamon together.
  2. Add the cubed bread to the egg mixture. Stir everything well to combine.
  3. Press the mixture into a greased 9x13" baking dish.
  4. For the topping, combine the brown sugar and 1/2 tsp cinnamon in a small bowl. Sprinkle over the bread.
  5. Cover and refrigerate for 8 hours or overnight.
  6. When ready to bake, preheat the oven to 350°F.
  7. Bake for 40-45 minutes, or until set.
  8. Let the baked French toast sit for 10 minutes before serving.
  9. Meanwhile, combine the maple cream syrup ingredients in a saucepan over medium-high heat.
  10. Bring the syrup to a boil, stirring frequently.
  11. Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  12. Cook until the syrup can coat the back of a spoon.
  13. Drizzle the maple cream syrup over each serving of baked French toast.
  14. Dust with powdered sugar, if desired.
