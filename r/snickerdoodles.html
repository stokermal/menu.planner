Snickerdoodles

Betty Crocker Cookbook, p. 181

30 min · about 4 dozen cookies servings · 90 cal · 1g protein

Note: Page is heavily splattered and clearly well used.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter or margarine, softened
  • 1/2 cup shortening
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons cream of tartar
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 400 degrees F.
  2. In a large bowl, beat 1 1/2 cups sugar, the butter, shortening, and eggs with an electric mixer on medium speed, or mix with a spoon. Stir in the flour, cream of tartar, baking soda, and salt.
  3. Shape the dough into 1 1/4-inch balls. In a small bowl, mix 1/4 cup sugar and the cinnamon. Roll the balls in the cinnamon-sugar mixture. On an ungreased cookie sheet, place the balls 2 inches apart.
  4. Bake 8 to 10 minutes or until set. Immediately remove from the cookie sheet to a wire rack.
