Meatball Subs

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 149

25 min · Serves 6 servings · 660 cal · 28g protein

Ingredients

  • 40 frozen Italian-style beef meatballs
  • 1 (28 oz) jar marinara sauce
  • 2 baguettes
  • 6 slices Provolone cheese
  • for garnish: shredded Parmesan cheese and fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Add the frozen meatballs and marinara sauce to a slow cooker.
  2. Cook on low for 6 hours or on high for 4 hours.
  3. Slice the baguettes to your desired sandwich size.
  4. Add the meatballs and a slice of Provolone to the inside of each sub.
  5. Place the open subs on a sheet pan and broil until the cheese is melted.
  6. Garnish with Parmesan and parsley, if desired.
