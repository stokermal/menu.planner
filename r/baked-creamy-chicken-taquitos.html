Baked Creamy Chicken Taquitos

Our Best Bites, p. 108

40 min · Makes 14-16 corn tortillas or 10-12 flour tortillas servings · 360 cal · 17g protein

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup (3 ounces) cream cheese
  • 1/4 cup green salsa
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon granulated garlic
  • 3 tablespoons chopped cilantro
  • 2 tablespoons chopped green onions
  • 2 cups cooked, shredded chicken
  • 1 cup shredded pepper jack cheese
  • 6-inch corn or flour tortillas (as fresh as possible; the longer they sit, the more likely they are to crack)
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil, optional
  • Nonstick cooking spray
  • Kosher salt

Steps

  1. Cook the chicken first if you are not using leftovers. Instant Pot: 1 1/2 cups chicken broth, 12 minutes on high pressure, natural release, then shred.
  2. Heat oven to 425 degrees F. Line a baking sheet with aluminum foil and spray lightly with cooking spray.
  3. Heat cream cheese in the microwave 20-30 seconds so it is soft and easy to stir. Add green salsa, lime juice, cumin, chili powder, onion powder, and granulated garlic. Stir to combine, then add cilantro, green onions, chicken, and pepper jack cheese.
  4. Wrap 3-4 tortillas at a time in damp paper towels and microwave 20-30 seconds so they are soft and pliable. If tortillas crack when rolled, use damper paper towels and increase microwave time.
  5. Place 2-3 tablespoons of the chicken mixture on the lower third of each tortilla, keeping it about 1/2 inch from the edges, then roll it up.
  6. Place seam-side down on the baking sheet in a single layer. Spray tops lightly with cooking spray or brush with vegetable oil. Sprinkle with a pinch of kosher salt. Bake 15-20 minutes or until crisp and the ends start to turn golden brown. Cool 5-10 minutes, then serve with sour cream, guacamole, or salsa.
