Ramen Noodle Bowls

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 49

10 min · Serves 6 servings · 280 cal · 13g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 8 cups chicken stock
  • 4 bricks ramen (10 oz total)
  • 2 cups spinach, chopped
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • 6 soft-boiled eggs
  • for garnish: sesame seeds

Steps

  1. Heat the sesame oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the garlic and saute until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  2. Add the chicken stock and bring to a boil.
  3. Add the ramen noodles and boil for 3 minutes.
  4. Add the spinach and season with salt and pepper, to taste.
  5. Ladle the ramen into a bowl and serve with a soft-boiled egg.
  6. Garnish with sesame seeds, if desired.
