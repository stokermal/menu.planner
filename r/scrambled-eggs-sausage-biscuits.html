Scrambled Eggs, Sausage, and Biscuits

House recipe

25 min · about 6 servings · 560 cal · 30g protein

Ingredients

  • 18 large eggs
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 pound breakfast sausage, links or patties
  • 2 cans Pillsbury refrigerated biscuits
  • Salt and pepper
  • Optional: shredded cheese for the eggs, jam or honey for the biscuits

Steps

  1. Start the biscuits first — they take the longest and everything else is fast. Bake per the package, usually 400 degrees F for 12 to 15 minutes.
  2. Cook the sausage on a sheet pan in the same oven, 400 degrees F for 15 to 18 minutes, turning once. Less mess than the stovetop and it frees the burner for the eggs.
  3. Whisk the eggs with a pinch of salt. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium-LOW heat — low and slow is what makes scrambled eggs creamy instead of rubbery.
  4. Pour in the eggs and let them sit 20 seconds before stirring. Push the set edges gently toward the middle and let the liquid run underneath. Keep the curds large.
  5. Pull the eggs off the heat while they still look slightly underdone and glossy. They finish in the pan, and eggs cooked until they look done in the skillet are overcooked on the plate.
  6. Season at the end, not the beginning. Salting raw eggs and letting them sit draws out water and makes them weep.
  7. Serve everything together with cheese and jam on the table.
