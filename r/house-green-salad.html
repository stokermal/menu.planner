House Green Salad

House recipe

15 min · 6 as a side servings · 45 cal · 2g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 romaine hearts (about 10 cups), chopped
  • 2 carrots, shredded
  • 1 English cucumber, halved lengthwise and sliced
  • 1/2 red onion, sliced thin
  • Buttermilk Ranch Dressing or Creamy Italian Dressing, to serve

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the romaine thoroughly. Wet greens will not hold dressing and go limp within minutes — a salad spinner or a clean towel is the whole trick.
  2. Soak the sliced red onion in cold water for 10 minutes, then drain. This pulls the harsh bite out so nobody picks it back off their plate.
  3. Shred the carrots on the large holes of a box grater rather than slicing them into coins. More surface area means carrot in every bite.
  4. Combine the romaine, carrots, cucumber, and drained onion in a large bowl.
  5. Serve undressed with the dressing alongside. Dress each plate as you eat so the rest keeps in the fridge for tomorrow.
