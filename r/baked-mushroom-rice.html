Baked Mushroom Rice

Our Best Bites, p. 200

75 min · Serves 8 servings · 220 cal · 5g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 cup onion, chopped
  • 1 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 3 cups beef broth
  • 1/2 cup apple juice
  • 1 tablespoon white vinegar
  • 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 cups uncooked long-grain rice

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F. Spray a 9 x 13-inch dish with nonstick cooking spray and set aside.
  2. Melt the butter over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook, stirring frequently, for about 2 minutes. Add the mushrooms and continue cooking until the onions are translucent and the mushrooms are tender. Remove from heat and combine the remaining ingredients in the pan.
  3. Transfer the mixture to the prepared 9 x 13-inch dish. Cover tightly with aluminum foil and bake for 45-60 minutes or until the liquid is absorbed. Stir before serving.
