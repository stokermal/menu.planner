Mini Huevos Rancheros

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 27

40 min · Serves 6 servings · 410 cal · 22g protein

Note: Yum. Very Filling!

Ingredients

  • 12 (6-inch) flour tortillas
  • 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1/4 cup salsa
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • 12 eggs
  • 1 cup shredded Mexican cheese
  • for serving: salsa, sour cream, avocado, and cilantro

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Spray a 12-ct muffin pan with nonstick spray.
  2. Trim the tortillas down to 6-inch circles, if yours are larger.
  3. Spray both sides of the tortillas with nonstick cooking spray and press them into the muffin pan, creasing on the sides to form tortilla cups.
  4. In a bowl, combine the black beans and salsa, then mash them with a fork. Season with salt and pepper. Divide the mixture evenly in the tortilla cups, then smooth with a spoon.
  5. Crack an egg into each cup. Season each egg with salt and pepper, then sprinkle with the cheese.
  6. Bake for 20-25 minutes, until the eggs are set. Let the egg cups stand for 5 minutes, then gently lift them from the pan.
  7. Serve with salsa, sour cream, avocado, and cilantro, if desired.
