Everything Avocado Toast

Love Real Food, p. 31

5 min · 2 avocado toasts servings · 240 cal · 6g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 slices whole-grain bread
  • 1 large or 2 small avocados, halved and pitted
  • Fine sea salt
  • Everything Spice Blend (sunflower seeds, sesame seeds, poppy seeds, dried onion, and more; see the book's blend recipe)

Steps

  1. Toast the bread in the toaster until golden.
  2. Using a spoon, scoop the flesh of the avocado onto a plate. Add a dash of salt and use a fork to mash until mostly smooth.
  3. Scoop the mashed avocado onto the bread and spread it into an even layer. Top with a sprinkle of Everything Spice Blend and serve immediately.
