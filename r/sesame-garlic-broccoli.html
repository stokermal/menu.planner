Sesame-Garlic Broccoli

House recipe

30 min · about 6 as a side servings · 125 cal · 5g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds broccoli (about 2 heads), cut into florets
  • 2 tablespoons neutral oil
  • 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon sesame seeds
  • Optional: pinch of red pepper flakes

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425 degrees F.
  2. Toss the florets with the neutral oil and spread on a sheet pan with space between them. Hold back the sesame oil, garlic and soy sauce for now — sesame oil scorches and garlic burns bitter over a full roast.
  3. Roast 18 to 20 minutes, until the edges are browned and crisp.
  4. Whisk the sesame oil, garlic, soy sauce and rice vinegar together while it roasts.
  5. Toss the hot broccoli with the sauce straight out of the oven. The residual heat cooks the raw garlic just enough without burning it, and the pan steam helps it coat.
  6. Scatter the sesame seeds and red pepper flakes over the top and serve.
