Tangy Red Bell Pepper Dressing

Love Real Food, p. 52

5 min · about 1 cup servings · 75 cal · 0g protein

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 large red bell pepper, sliced into roughly 2-inch pieces
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup
  • 1 teaspoon apple cider vinegar (omit for a sweeter dressing)
  • 1 clove garlic, roughly chopped
  • 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • Pinch of red pepper flakes

Steps

  1. In a blender, combine all of the ingredients and blend until smooth.
  2. Taste and add another pinch of salt if the dressing needs a little something more.
