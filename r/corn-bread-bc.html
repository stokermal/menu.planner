Corn Bread

Betty Crocker Cookbook, p. 79

35 min · 12 servings servings · 175 cal · 4g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup butter or margarine, melted
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 1/4 cups yellow, white, or blue cornmeal
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 400 degrees F. Grease the bottom and side of a 9-inch round pan or an 8-inch square pan with shortening, or spray with cooking spray.
  2. In a large bowl, beat the milk, butter, and egg with a hand beater or wire whisk. Stir in the remaining ingredients all at once, just until the flour is moistened. The batter will be lumpy. Pour into the pan.
  3. Bake 20 to 25 minutes or until golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Serve warm if desired.
