Sheet Pan Quesadillas

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 125

45 min · Serves 6-8 servings · 635 cal · 32g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 2 Tbsp taco seasoning
  • 14 flour tortillas
  • 3 cups shredded Mexican cheese
  • for topping: salsa, sour cream, avocado, cilantro, and lime wedges

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat and add the ground beef and onion. Break apart the meat and cook until the onion is translucent and the meat is cooked through. Drain the excess grease.
  3. Add the black beans and taco seasoning to the skillet. Break down the beans so they blend well with the meat.
  4. Spray a sheet pan with nonstick spray. Overlap tortillas on the bottom of the sheet pan. Tortillas on the edge should hang halfway over the pan.
  5. Spread 1 1/2 cups of shredded cheese over the tortillas. Add the ground beef mixture and top with the remaining 1 1/2 cups of cheese.
  6. Fold overlapping portions of the tortillas onto the filling mixture. Add additional tortillas to cover the entire top.
  7. Spray the top of the tortillas with nonstick spray and place another sheet pan on top of the whole quesadilla to keep everything in place while baking.
  8. Bake for 20-25 minutes, until the tortillas are crispy.
  9. Remove from the oven and slice into squares with a pizza cutter.
  10. Top with salsa, sour cream, avocado, cilantro, and lime wedges, if desired.
