Bacon-Wrapped Teriyaki Chicken Skewers

Our Best Bites, p. 117

50 min · 8-10 skewers or 24-30 appetizers servings · 285 cal · 24g protein

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces bacon (not thick-sliced)
  • 1 pound boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 (16-ounce) can pineapple chunks
  • 1 recipe Teriyaki Sauce (p. 46)

Steps

  1. Cut the bacon into thirds. Count how many pieces you have and keep that in mind when cutting up the chicken breasts.
  2. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces; you'll want roughly as many pieces of chicken as you have of bacon.
  3. Drain the pineapple and place in a bowl or on a plate to avoid reaching into the can repeatedly. Set up a workstation with toothpicks or kabob skewers, chicken, bacon, and pineapple, plus a shallow dish or heavy-duty zip-top bag for marinating.
  4. Wrap a piece of bacon around a piece of chicken and secure with a toothpick. If making larger skewers, thread the meat all the way to the bottom. Top with a chunk of pineapple. For skewers, repeat twice so there are three pieces of bacon-wrapped chicken on the skewer. Repeat with the remaining chicken, bacon, and pineapple.
  5. Reserve 1/2 cup of Teriyaki Sauce and pour the remaining sauce over the chicken. Marinate for 4-8 hours.
  6. To grill: preheat the grill to medium-high heat (about 400 degrees F). Place skewers on the grill and baste with the reserved Teriyaki Sauce. Cook for 7 minutes, turn, baste, and cook for another 7 minutes.
  7. To bake: preheat the oven to 400 degrees F and place skewers on a foil-lined baking sheet. Cook for 20 minutes, turning and basting once.
