Greek-Inspired Spring Mix Salad

House recipe

15 min · 6 as a side servings · 140 cal · 6g protein

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces spring mix (about 8 cups)
  • 1 cup cooked beets, diced
  • 3/4 cup kalamata olives, halved
  • 1/2 cup pepperoncini, drained and sliced
  • 6 ounces feta cheese, crumbled
  • Greek Red Wine Vinaigrette, to serve

Steps

  1. Spread the spring mix in a large shallow bowl. Spring mix bruises easily, so use a wide bowl rather than a deep one and do not pack it down.
  2. Add the beets last and keep them to one side rather than stirring them through, or they will stain everything pink.
  3. Scatter the olives, pepperoncini, and feta over the top.
  4. Serve with the vinaigrette alongside. Dress each plate as you eat — spring mix wilts within minutes once dressed, and undressed this holds through tomorrow.
