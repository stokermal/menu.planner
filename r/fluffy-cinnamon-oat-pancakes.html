Fluffy Cinnamon Oat Pancakes

Love Real Food, p. 18

30 min · 7 or 8 medium pancakes, enough for 2 to 3 servings servings · 330 cal · 10g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 cup milk of choice
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar or distilled white vinegar
  • 1 cup white whole wheat flour or regular whole wheat flour
  • 1/3 cup quick-cooking oats
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 tablespoons maple syrup or honey
  • 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract

Steps

  1. In a 2-cup liquid measuring cup, combine the milk and vinegar. Stir and let this homemade buttermilk mixture rest until lightly curdled, at least 5 minutes.
  2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, oats, baking powder, cinnamon, baking soda, and salt.
  3. To the buttermilk mixture, whisk in the egg, melted butter, maple syrup, and vanilla until thoroughly blended.
  4. Pour the liquid mixture into the flour mixture. Stir just until combined; a few small lumps are okay. Let the batter rest 5 minutes so the pancakes will be nice and fluffy.
  5. Heat a cast iron skillet or nonstick griddle over medium-low, or preheat an electric skillet to 375 degrees F. Lightly oil if needed.
  6. Gently stir the batter one last time in case the liquid has separated. Using a 1/3-cup measure, scoop a scant 1/3 cup onto the skillet. The batter is thick, so spread it a bit with the back of your measuring cup.
  7. Cook until small bubbles form on the surface, 2 to 3 minutes. It is ready to flip when about 1/2 inch of the perimeter is matte. Flip and cook 1 to 2 minutes more until lightly golden.
  8. Repeat with the remaining batter. Serve immediately or keep warm in a 200 degree F oven.
  9. Leftovers keep refrigerated up to 3 days or frozen up to 2 months. Reheat stacked and wrapped in a paper towel in the microwave.
