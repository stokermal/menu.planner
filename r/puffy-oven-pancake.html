Puffy Oven Pancake

Betty Crocker Cookbook

40 min · 2 to 4 servings servings · 315 cal · 12g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons butter or margarine
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • Lemon juice and powdered sugar, or cut-up fruit, if desired

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 400 degrees F. In a 9-inch pie plate, melt the butter in the oven, then brush it over the bottom and side of the plate.
  2. In a medium bowl, beat the eggs slightly with a wire whisk or hand beater. Beat in the flour, milk, and salt just until mixed. Do not overbeat or the pancake may not puff. Pour into the pie plate.
  3. Bake 25 to 30 minutes or until puffy and deep golden brown. Serve immediately, sprinkled with lemon juice and powdered sugar or topped with fruit.
