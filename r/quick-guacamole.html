Quick Guacamole

Love Real Food, p. 34

5 min · about 1 1/4 cups servings · 130 cal · 2g protein

Note: YUM!

Ingredients

  • 2 avocados, halved and pitted
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice, or more if needed
  • Kosher salt

Steps

  1. Using a spoon, scoop the flesh of the avocados into a small bowl. Add the lime juice and 1/4 teaspoon salt and mash with a pastry cutter, potato masher, or fork until the mixture is blended and no longer chunky. Taste and add additional lime juice and/or salt if needed.
