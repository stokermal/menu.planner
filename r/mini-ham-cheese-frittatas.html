Mini Ham and Cheese Frittatas

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 33

22 min · Serves 6-8 (makes 24 mini) servings · 120 cal · 10g protein

Ingredients

  • 8 eggs
  • 1/3 cup milk
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp pepper
  • 4 slices ham, finely diced
  • 1/2 cup spinach, finely chopped
  • 1/3 cup Parmesan cheese, finely shredded

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Spray a 24-ct mini muffin pan with nonstick spray.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, salt, and pepper. Stir in the ham, spinach, and Parmesan.
  3. Fill the mini muffin cups with the egg mixture, almost to the top.
  4. Bake for 10-12 minutes, until the eggs are set.
