Classic Sunday Roast

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 151

90 min · Serves 6 servings · 520 cal · 38g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 (3-5 lb) beef chuck roast
  • 2 Tbsp canola or avocado oil
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 1 lb baby carrots
  • 1 onion, roughly diced
  • 2 cups water
  • 2 tsp Better Than Bouillon Beef Base
  • 1/4 cup Worcestershire sauce

Steps

  1. Heat a large, heavy-bottomed skillet over medium-high heat and add the oil.
  2. Pat the beef dry and season both sides with the salt, pepper, garlic powder, and thyme.
  3. Sear both sides of the beef until a golden crust has formed, 4-5 minutes per side.
  4. Transfer the beef to the slow cooker.
  5. Layer in the potatoes, carrots and onion.
  6. In a bowl, whisk together the water, beef base, and Worcestershire sauce. Pour the mixture over the beef.
  7. Cook on low for 8 hours or on high for 4-5 hours.
  8. Pull apart the meat and serve in bowls with plenty of sauce and vegetables.
