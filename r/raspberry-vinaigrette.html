Raspberry Vinaigrette

Betty Crocker Cookbook, p. 400

10 min · 1 cup vinaigrette servings · 45 cal · 0g protein

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup seedless raspberry jam
  • 1/2 cup red wine vinegar
  • 1/4 cup olive or vegetable oil
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. In a small glass or plastic bowl, beat all of the ingredients with a wire whisk until well blended.
  2. Store tightly covered in the refrigerator.
