Apple Cider Syrup

Our Best Bites, p. 69

13 min · Makes 2 1/2 cups servings · 55 cal · 0g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 cups apple juice
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cloves

Steps

  1. Combine all the ingredients and cook over medium heat just until thickened and clear. Refrigerate the leftovers for up to 1 week.
