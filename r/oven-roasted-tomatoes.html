Oven-Roasted Tomatoes

Our Best Bites, p. 193

175 min · Serving size varies servings · 90 cal · 1g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds tomatoes, any variety
  • 4 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
  • 2 tablespoons minced garlic
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon fresh, coarsely ground black pepper
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons sugar

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees F. and line a rimmed cookie sheet with foil.
  2. Cut your tomatoes: halve small grape or Roma tomatoes; quarter anything larger.
  3. Place the cut tomatoes on the prepared baking sheet and drizzle with 3 tablespoons olive oil. Toss with hands until all tomatoes are well coated and arrange in a single layer, skin sides down.
  4. Sprinkle minced garlic over the tomatoes. Sprinkle evenly with salt, pepper, and sugar. Drizzle the remaining tablespoon of oil over the tomatoes.
  5. Cook for 2 1/2-3 hours, or until the juices have dried up and some tomatoes start to brown around the edges. Leftovers can be frozen whole in a zip-top bag or pureed and frozen in 3- or 6-ounce portions.
