Cilantro-Lime Vinaigrette

Our Best Bites, p. 88

5 min · 2 cups servings · 125 cal · 0g protein

Note: DOUBLE! written on the page in marker, plus a checkmark and a page flag.

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup fresh lime juice (about 2-3 juicy limes)
  • 2 teaspoons sugar
  • 1/4 cup white wine vinegar or rice vinegar
  • 1 cup canola oil
  • 4-5 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 cup roughly chopped cilantro, stems removed
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher or sea salt

Steps

  1. In the jar of your blender, combine the lime juice, vinegar, garlic, salt, and sugar. Blend until the ingredients are completely combined.
  2. With the blender running, add the oil in a steady stream. Add the cilantro and blend until the cilantro has broken down but still maintains some of its texture.
  3. If possible, refrigerate at least 4 hours before serving. Serve with greens, on any type of Mexican salad, or use as a marinade.
