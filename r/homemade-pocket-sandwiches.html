Homemade Pocket Sandwiches

Our Best Bites, p. 31

100 min · 8 sandwiches servings · 340 cal · 17g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 recipe Breadstick Dough (p. 30)
  • Desired fillings: about 6-8 ounces of deli meat
  • 4-6 ounces of shredded cheese

Steps

  1. Prepare the Breadstick Dough through the first rise. After the dough has risen the first time, turn it onto a lightly floured surface and roll into a rectangle 16 inches long and 8 inches wide. Using a pizza cutter, cut the dough into 8 equal portions.
  2. Leaving a little margin on the right and left sides, add the toppings, with the cheese going on last. Stretch the dough out a little to give yourself a bit more to work with. Fold the left side over the middle and then the right side over that. Starting at the bottom of the 'mummy,' tightly roll the filled dough, stretching it very gently as you go to make sure you're getting a tight seal. Place on a sprayed cookie sheet and repeat with the remaining dough.
  3. When finished, cover the pan with a clean cloth and preheat your oven to 425 degrees F.
  4. When the oven has heated, bake the pockets for about 15 minutes or until golden brown on top. For shine, brush the tops with a bit of egg white mixed with water during the last 5 minutes of baking, or rub a little butter on top of the rolls while they're still hot.
