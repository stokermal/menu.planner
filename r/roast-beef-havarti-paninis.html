Roast Beef and Havarti Paninis

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 117

25 min · Serves 6 servings · 505 cal · 28g protein

Note: YUM!

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf French bread
  • 2 Tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 8 slices Havarti cheese (about 1/2 lb)
  • 14 slices roast beef (about 1 lb)
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup spring salad mix

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
  2. Slice the entire loaf of French bread in half lengthwise. Assemble as one giant sandwich with a thin layer of Dijon, Havarti, roast beef, tomato, red onion, and spring salad mix.
  3. Place the sandwich on a sheet pan and top with another sheet pan.
  4. Place in the oven with a heavy, oven-safe pot or cast iron skillet on the top sheet pan to press the sandwich down.
  5. Bake for 15 minutes, until the cheese is melted and the bread is toasty.
  6. Once cooled slightly, slice into smaller sandwiches.
