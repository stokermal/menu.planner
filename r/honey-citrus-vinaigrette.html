Honey-Citrus Vinaigrette

Our Best Bites, p. 91

5 min · 1 1/4 cups servings · 115 cal · 0g protein

Note: Checkmarked in the book.

Ingredients

  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 3/4 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons grated onion
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/3 cup fresh orange juice
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 3 tablespoons fresh lime juice
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 tablespoons honey

Steps

  1. Combine all the ingredients together in a jar and shake until well combined.
  2. Shake well again before serving.
