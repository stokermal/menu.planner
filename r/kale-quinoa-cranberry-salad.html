Kale and Quinoa Salad with Crisp Celery, Plumped Cranberries, and Lemon Dressing

Love Real Food, p. 59

35 min · 4 medium salads servings · 370 cal · 11g protein

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup quinoa, rinsed
  • 1 cup water
  • 1/3 cup dried cranberries or dried tart cherries
  • Dressing: 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • Dressing: 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • Dressing: 2 teaspoons Dijon mustard
  • Dressing: 2 teaspoons maple syrup or honey
  • Dressing: 1 large clove garlic, pressed or minced
  • Dressing: 1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • Dressing: several twists of freshly ground black pepper
  • Salad: 1 medium bunch curly kale (about 8 ounces), ribs removed and leaves chopped
  • Salad: fine sea salt
  • Salad: 2 ribs celery, chopped
  • Salad: 1/2 cup sliced almonds
  • Salad: 1/2 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • Salad: 1/3 cup crumbled goat cheese (about 2 ounces)

Steps

  1. In a small saucepan, combine the rinsed quinoa and water. Bring to a boil over medium-high, then reduce to a gentle simmer. Simmer uncovered until all the water is absorbed, 11 to 14 minutes. Off the heat, stir in the dried cranberries so they plump, and cover. Let steam 10 minutes, then fluff with a fork and set aside.
  2. Meanwhile, to make the dressing: in a small bowl, combine the olive oil, lemon juice, mustard, maple syrup, garlic, salt, and pepper and whisk until blended.
  3. To assemble: place the chopped kale in a big salad bowl. Sprinkle a small pinch of salt over it and massage the leaves by scrunching big handfuls at a time, until the leaves darken, become fragrant, and reduce in volume by about a third. Add the celery.
  4. In a small skillet, combine the almonds and olive oil and cook over medium, stirring frequently, until fragrant and golden on the edges, 5 to 8 minutes. Almonds burn easily, so watch them. Pour into the salad bowl.
  5. While the quinoa mixture is warm, add it to the bowl. Drizzle in all the dressing and toss to coat. Wait until the salad has cooled to nearly room temperature before gently tossing in the goat cheese so it does not melt. Serve immediately. Keeps well chilled up to 4 days.
