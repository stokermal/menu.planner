Sweet and Sour Appetizer Meatballs

Our Best Bites, p. 19

185 min · Makes 16-20 appetizer servings servings · 170 cal · 6g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 (12-ounce) jar chili sauce
  • 1 (16-ounce) jar grape jelly
  • 2 pounds frozen meatballs

Steps

  1. Combine the jelly and chili sauce in a slow cooker and stir until smooth. Heat the mixture if needed to combine. Add the meatballs and set the temperature to low. Cook for 2-5 hours on low. Serve with toothpicks.
