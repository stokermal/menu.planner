Olive Garden Copycat Salad

House recipe

20 min · 6 as a side servings · 110 cal · 4g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 large head romaine (about 8 cups), chopped
  • 3 cups shredded red cabbage
  • 1 cup shredded carrot
  • 1 cup grape or cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 red onion, sliced into thin rings
  • 1/2 cup sliced black olives, drained
  • 1/2 cup pepperoncini, drained
  • 1/2 cup shaved Parmesan cheese
  • 1 1/2 cups croutons
  • Creamy Italian Dressing, to serve

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the romaine and red cabbage thoroughly.
  2. Combine the romaine, red cabbage, carrot, tomatoes, and onion rings in a large bowl. Roughly a 3-to-1 ratio of romaine to cabbage gives real crunch and color without the cabbage taking over.
  3. Scatter the olives, pepperoncini, and shaved Parmesan over the top.
  4. Add the croutons at the very last moment, or keep them on the side. Croutons in a dressed salad turn to sponge within minutes.
  5. Serve with the dressing alongside and dress each plate.
