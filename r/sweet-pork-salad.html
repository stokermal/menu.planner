Sweet Pork Salad

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 153

120 min · Serves 8-10 servings · 590 cal · 28g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 (3-4 lb) pork butt roast, trimmed of excess fat
  • 1 Tbsp garlic salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 1 (32 oz) jar salsa
  • 1 cup brown sugar, packed
  • 3-4 heads of romaine lettuce, chopped
  • 4 cups grape tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 (16 oz) bottle cilantro lime dressing
  • for serving: guacamole, shredded Mexican cheese, and tortilla chips

Steps

  1. Place the pork roast in the slow cooker. Sprinkle with the garlic salt and pepper.
  2. In a bowl, stir together the salsa and brown sugar. Pour the mixture over the pork.
  3. Cook on low for 9-10 hours or on high for 6 hours, until the pork is falling apart. Shred the pork in the cooking liquid.
  4. Assemble the salad by layering the romaine lettuce, shredded pork, and tomatoes.
  5. Serve with dressing, guacamole, shredded cheese, and tortilla chips, if desired.
