Roasted Sausage and Vegetables

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 109

45 min · Serves 6-8 servings · 355 cal · 13g protein

Ingredients

  • 5 links (1 1/2 lb) Aidell's Smoked Chicken Sausage, sliced
  • 2 medium sweet potatoes, cubed
  • 1 lb brussel sprouts, halved
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 2 tsp Italian Spice & Salt (or 1 tsp salt and 1 tsp Italian seasoning)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
  2. Place the sausage and vegetables on a sheet pan. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with Italian Spice & Salt. Toss to coat evenly. Spread the sausage and vegetables out in a single layer.
  3. Bake for 30 minutes, until the vegetables are tender.
