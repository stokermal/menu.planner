Instant Pot Shredded Chicken (House Prep)

House Recipe

35 min · 3 lb raw yields about 8 cups shredded servings · 165 cal · 31g protein

Ingredients

  • 3 pounds boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 cup chicken broth (or water)
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • SEASONING - pick the profile that matches what you're making (see variations)

Steps

  1. Pat the chicken breasts dry and season on both sides with salt plus your chosen seasoning profile.
  2. Pour 1 cup broth into the 8-quart Instant Pot Duo. Add the chicken in as close to a single layer as you can, overlapping a little is fine.
  3. Seal the lid, set the valve to Sealing, and cook on High Pressure for 10 minutes. For a batch over 4 pounds, or if the breasts are unusually thick, use 12 minutes.
  4. When the timer finishes, let it natural release for 10 minutes, then quick release the remaining pressure.
  5. Check the thickest breast with a thermometer. You want 165 degrees F. If it's short, reseal and cook 2 more minutes.
  6. Shred with two forks, or drop the breasts in a stand mixer with the paddle and run it on low for 20-30 seconds.
  7. Return the shredded chicken to the pot and toss with some of the cooking liquid. This is the step that keeps it from going dry in the fridge. Cool and portion within 2 hours.
