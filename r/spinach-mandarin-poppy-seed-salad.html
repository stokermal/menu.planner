Spinach Mandarin Poppy Seed Salad

Our Best Bites, p. 76

25 min · 8-10 side dish servings servings · 245 cal · 12g protein

Note: Checkmarked in the book.

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces (1/2 bag) baby spinach
  • 1/2 to 1 head Romaine lettuce, chopped
  • 1/2 thinly sliced medium red onion
  • 1 large avocado, cubed
  • 8 ounces mozzarella or Swiss cheese, shredded
  • 1 (12-ounce) package bacon, cooked and crumbled
  • 1 (12-ounce) can Mandarin oranges, drained
  • Poppy Seed Dressing (p. 90), to taste

Steps

  1. Combine all ingredients except the dressing in a large bowl.
  2. When ready to serve, toss with the desired amount of salad dressing and serve immediately.
  3. If you anticipate leftovers, let your eaters dress their own salads, because the dressing will make the greens wilt.
