Honeydew Green Smoothie

100 Days of Real Food: Fast & Fabulous, p. 161

7 min · Makes 3 or 4 servings servings · 90 cal · 1g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 bananas (the riper, the sweeter!)
  • 1 1/2 cups spinach leaves
  • 1 cup diced honeydew melon
  • 1 cup diced frozen or fresh pineapple
  • 1/2 cup ice cubes

Steps

  1. In a blender, combine 1 cup water, the bananas, spinach, honeydew, pineapple, and ice cubes, and blend until smooth. Serve immediately or store in fridge for up to 24 hours.
