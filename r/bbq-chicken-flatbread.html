BBQ Chicken Flatbread

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 51

20 min · Serves 6 servings · 570 cal · 40g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rotisserie chicken, chopped
  • 1 cup BBQ sauce, divided
  • 6 naan
  • 12 slices Mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup corn (fresh or frozen)
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • for garnish: cilantro

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
  2. In a bowl, add the chicken and 1/2 cup of the BBQ sauce. Stir to coat.
  3. Layer the naan on a sheet pan. Spread the remaining 1/2 cup of BBQ sauce on the naan.
  4. Remove the Mozzarella from the package and soak up any extra moisture with a paper towel.
  5. Top the naan with the cheese, BBQ chicken, corn, and red onion. Bake for 8-10 minutes.
  6. Garnish with cilantro, if desired.
