Brown Sugar Refrigerator Cookies

Betty Crocker Cookbook, p. 181

150 min · about 6 dozen cookies servings · 60 cal · 1g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 cup butter or margarine, softened
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1 large egg
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/3 cup chopped nuts

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, beat the brown sugar, butter, vanilla, and egg with an electric mixer on medium speed, or mix with a spoon. Stir in the remaining ingredients except the nuts, then stir in the nuts.
  2. Shape the dough into a 10 x 3-inch rectangle on plastic wrap. Wrap and refrigerate about 2 hours or until firm, but no longer than 24 hours.
  3. Heat the oven to 375 degrees F.
  4. Cut the rectangle into 1/8-inch slices. On an ungreased cookie sheet, place the slices about 1 inch apart.
  5. Bake 6 to 8 minutes or until light brown. Cool 1 to 2 minutes, then remove from the cookie sheet to a wire rack.
