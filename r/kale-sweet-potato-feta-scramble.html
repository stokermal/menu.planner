Kale, Sweet Potato, and Feta Scramble

Love Real Food, p. 42

35 min · 4 servings servings · 450 cal · 18g protein

Ingredients

  • Sweet potato and kale: 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • Sweet potato and kale: pinch of red pepper flakes
  • Sweet potato and kale: 1 pound sweet potatoes (about 2 small or 1 large), peeled and cut into 1/4-inch cubes
  • Sweet potato and kale: 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • Sweet potato and kale: 1/2 medium bunch kale (about 4 ounces), preferably Tuscan, ribs removed and leaves chopped
  • Sweet potato and kale: 1/2 cup water
  • Sweet potato and kale: 1/3 cup chopped green onion (2 to 4), plus more for garnish
  • Scrambled eggs: 8 eggs
  • Scrambled eggs: 2 tablespoons neutral-flavored milk of choice
  • Scrambled eggs: 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • Scrambled eggs: freshly ground black pepper
  • Scrambled eggs: 2 teaspoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • Garnishes: 1/3 cup crumbled feta cheese (about 2 ounces)
  • Garnishes: 1 avocado, sliced into thin strips
  • Garnishes: hot sauce

Steps

  1. To prepare the sweet potato and kale: in a large skillet, heat the olive oil over medium until shimmering. Add the pepper flakes and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Add the sweet potatoes and salt and toss to coat. Add a few handfuls of kale and toss to coat in the oil. Repeat until all the kale is in the pan.
  2. Pour the water into the pan, avoiding oil splatters. Cover, reduce heat to medium-low, and cook 5 minutes. Uncover and toss in the green onions. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the liquid has evaporated and the sweet potatoes are tender, 9 to 11 minutes. Set aside.
  3. Meanwhile, to scramble the eggs: crack the eggs into a medium bowl. Add the milk, salt, and a few twists of pepper. Beat with a whisk until thoroughly combined and pure yellow.
  4. In a medium skillet, warm the oil over medium-high until shimmering. Swirl to coat and pour in the egg mixture. Scrape the eggs along the bottom and sides until they begin to clump and the spatula leaves a trail, about 1 1/2 minutes.
  5. Reduce heat to low and gently fold the eggs over on themselves until clumpy but still slightly wet, 30 to 60 seconds. Scoot the eggs into the pan of cooked sweet potato and kale. Stir to combine, then season to taste.
  6. Spoon into bowls and garnish each with feta, a few slices of avocado, and chopped green onion. Serve immediately with hot sauce on the side.
