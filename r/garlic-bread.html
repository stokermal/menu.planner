Garlic Bread

House recipe

15 min · about 10 slices servings · 210 cal · 5g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf store-bought French bread (or sourdough), split lengthwise
  • 1/2 cup salted butter, softened
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons Garlic Bread Seasoning (p. 53)

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 400 degrees F.
  2. Stir the Garlic Bread Seasoning into the softened butter. This ratio is the one printed with the seasoning recipe, and it is enough for a full loaf.
  3. Split the loaf lengthwise and spread the butter edge to edge. The corners are what people fight over, so do not leave a dry border.
  4. Bake cut side up for 8 to 10 minutes, until the butter has soaked in and the edges are golden.
  5. For a crisper top, broil for the last 60 to 90 seconds. Do not walk away — it goes from golden to burnt in under a minute.
  6. Slice and serve hot.
