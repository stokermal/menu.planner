Ranch Chicken

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 121

40 min · Serves 6 servings · 370 cal · 23g protein

Ingredients

  • 6 boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 1 lb baby carrots
  • 1/4 cup avocado or olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp dry ranch mix
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, combine all of the ingredients, then stir to coat evenly.
  3. Pour the mixture onto a sheet pan.
  4. Bake for 25-30 minutes, until the internal temperature of the chicken reaches 165°F.
  5. Broil an additional 2-3 minutes, until the chicken is caramelized and just browning.
