Honey-Lime Dip

Our Best Bites, p. 180

5 min · Makes about 1 cup servings · 40 cal · 1g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 (6-ounce) container plain, low-fat yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon mayonnaise
  • 1/2 tablespoon honey
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lime juice
  • 1/4 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/8 teaspoon oregano
  • 1 teaspoon parsley
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt

Steps

  1. Whisk together all the ingredients and chill until ready to serve.
