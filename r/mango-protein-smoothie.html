Mango Protein Smoothie

House recipe

5 min · about 6 cups, 6 servings servings · 265 cal · 20g protein

Ingredients

  • 4 cups frozen mango chunks
  • 2 bananas
  • 2 scoops vanilla protein powder
  • 1 1/2 cups plain Greek yogurt
  • 3 cups milk
  • 1/2 cup rolled oats (optional)
  • Honey to taste, only if the fruit needs it

Steps

  1. Put the liquid in first — milk, then yogurt. Every blender works better with the liquid at the bottom, and it stops the frozen fruit jamming the blades.
  2. Add the bananas, then the protein powder, then the frozen mango on top.
  3. Blend on high 45 to 60 seconds, until completely smooth. A high-powered blender handles the frozen fruit straight from the bag with no thawing.
  4. Taste before adding any honey. Ripe mango and banana are usually sweet enough, and protein powder is already sweetened.
  5. For a thicker, spoonable version use 2 cups of milk instead of 3.
