Chili-Lime Steak

Our Best Bites, p. 124

24 min · Makes seasoning for 1-2 pounds of steak servings · 255 cal · 33g protein

Note: Yum! (next to the rub). Also yum! (next to the salad variation).

Ingredients

  • 1-2 pounds boneless steak (flank steak works well)
  • Rub: 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • Rub: 1 teaspoon granulated garlic
  • Rub: 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • Rub: 1/2 teaspoon coriander
  • Rub: 1/2 teaspoon oregano
  • Rub: 1/8-1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (chipotle chili powder may be substituted for a smoky rub)
  • Rub: 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt
  • Rub: 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 lime, juiced (about 2 tablespoons lime juice)
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil

Steps

  1. For the rub, combine spices in a small bowl. Add lime juice and olive oil and stir to combine.
  2. Place the steak in a shallow dish (such as a 9 x 13-inch baking dish). Pour the spice mixture over the steak and rub it in with your hands. Let the steak stand 15 minutes. While the meat stands, preheat your grill.
  3. Place the steak on the grill over medium-high heat and cook 5-7 minutes per side or until desired doneness. Remove from grill and let stand 5 minutes before slicing.
  4. Slice steak against the grain into strips about 1/4-inch thick.
