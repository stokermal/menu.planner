Sweet Potato Fries

Our Best Bites, p. 180

50 min · Serves 4-6 servings · 125 cal · 2g protein

Ingredients

  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon coriander
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon parsley
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 pound peeled sweet potatoes cut into 1/4-inch matchsticks
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 425 degrees F.
  2. In a small bowl, combine cumin, oregano, coriander, salt, parsley, and pepper.
  3. Place the sweet potatoes in a pile directly on a baking sheet and drizzle with olive oil. Use your hands to toss until all pieces are well coated. Sprinkle seasoning mixture on top and toss again to coat.
  4. Arrange sweet potatoes in a single layer so pieces are not touching. Bake for 15 minutes. Use a metal spatula to gently flip fries and return the pan to the oven. Cook an additional 15-20 minutes or until starting to lightly brown and crisp. Remove and cool 5 minutes. Serve immediately with Honey-Lime Dip.
