Oven-Baked Steak Fries and Fry Sauce

Our Best Bites, p. 182

50 min · Serves 4-6 servings · 245 cal · 4g protein

Note: Checkmarked in the book.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 medium russet potatoes, washed
  • FRY SAUCE:
  • 1/4 cup real mayonnaise (no Miracle Whip)
  • 1/4 cup ketchup
  • 2-3 teaspoons dill pickle relish

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F. Mix the spices in a medium bowl. Add the olive oil and combine well.
  2. Cut the potatoes into 8 wedges each. Add the potato wedges to the seasonings and toss to coat.
  3. Lightly crumple some aluminum foil and place it on a baking sheet. Spray the foil with nonstick cooking spray and then arrange the potato wedges on the foil. This makes cleanup easy because the potatoes won't stick, and it also ensures they cook evenly.
  4. Bake for about 40 minutes.
  5. For the fry sauce: whisk the mayonnaise, ketchup, and relish to combine and serve with the fries if desired.
