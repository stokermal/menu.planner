Breakfast Pizzas

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 29

22 min · Serves 6 servings · 560 cal · 28g protein

Ingredients

  • 6 breakfast sausage patties
  • 6 naan
  • 2 cups shredded Mexican cheese
  • 6 eggs
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • for serving: hot sauce

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat and add the sausage patties. Break apart the meat until it is cooked through.
  3. Place the naan on a lined sheet pan. Sprinkle the sausage evenly over the naan.
  4. Add a ring of shredded cheese around the outside of the naan to make a nest for the egg to lay in.
  5. Crack an egg into the center of each naan and sprinkle it with salt and pepper, to taste.
  6. Bake for 10-12 minutes, just until the egg is set.
  7. Serve with hot sauce, if desired.
