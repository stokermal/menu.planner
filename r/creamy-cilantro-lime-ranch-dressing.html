Creamy Cilantro-Lime Ranch Dressing

Our Best Bites, p. 85

5 min · about 2 cups servings · 100 cal · 1g protein

Note: Checkmarked and page-flagged in the book.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/2 cup milk or buttermilk
  • 1 lime, juiced (about 2 tablespoons lime juice)
  • 1 packet Hidden Valley Ranch dressing mix (ignore the directions on the packet)
  • 2 cloves garlic, roughly chopped
  • 1/2 cup roughly chopped cilantro
  • 1/4 cup green salsa (La Costena or Herdez brands are the most common)
  • Hot sauce (like Cholula or Tabasco), to taste

Steps

  1. Combine the mayonnaise, milk, lime juice, and the contents of the packet of ranch dressing in a blender.
  2. Add the garlic, cilantro, and green salsa and blend to thoroughly mix all the ingredients.
  3. Season with hot sauce, if desired.
  4. If possible, refrigerate for several hours to allow the dressing to thicken and the flavors to meld.
