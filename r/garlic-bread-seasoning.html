Garlic Bread Seasoning

Our Best Bites, p. 53

5 min · Makes about 1 cup servings · 10 cal · 1g protein

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup powdered Parmesan cheese
  • 2 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 2 tablespoons garlic powder
  • 2 teaspoons oregano
  • 2 teaspoons basil
  • 2 teaspoons marjoram
  • 2 teaspoons parsley

Steps

  1. Shake ingredients together in an airtight container. Store in the refrigerator for up to 3 months.
