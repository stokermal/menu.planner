Best Waffles Ever

Love Real Food, p. 21

30 min · three 7-inch round Belgian waffles or 5 thinner Eggo-style waffles servings · 765 cal · 17g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups oat flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • Pinch of ground cinnamon
  • 3/4 cup milk of choice
  • 5 tablespoons coconut oil or unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tablespoons maple syrup, plus more for serving
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps

  1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the oat flour, baking powder, salt, and cinnamon.
  2. In a small bowl, combine the milk, coconut oil, eggs, maple syrup, and vanilla. Whisk until well blended. If the coconut oil solidifies on contact with the cold ingredients, gently warm the mixture in 30-second intervals until it melts again.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the flour mixture. Stir just until combined; the batter will still be lumpy. Let the batter rest 10 minutes so the oat flour absorbs moisture. Preheat the waffle iron, set to high if it has a dial.
  4. Give the batter one more swirl in case the liquid separated. The batter will be thick. Pour enough onto the heated iron to cover the center and most of the surface, and close the lid. Wait to check until no more steam is rising.
  5. Cook until crisp and golden brown; you may need to go longer than the indicator light suggests. To hold, keep warm on a baking sheet in a 200 degree F oven without stacking.
  6. Repeat with the remaining batter. Leftovers refrigerate and freeze beautifully. Store in freezer bags and pop individual waffles into the toaster.
