Banana Nut Bread

Love Real Food, p. 22

85 min · 1 loaf servings · 277 cal · 6g protein

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup roughly chopped raw walnuts or pecans
  • 1/3 cup melted coconut oil or extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 cup honey or maple syrup
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup mashed overripe bananas (from about 2 1/2 medium or 2 large bananas)
  • 1/4 cup milk of choice or water
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon, plus more to swirl on top
  • 1 3/4 cups white whole wheat flour or regular whole wheat flour

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 325 degrees F. Line a small rimmed baking sheet with parchment. Grease a 9 x 5-inch loaf pan.
  2. Pour the chopped nuts onto the baking sheet and bake until fragrant and toasted, about 5 minutes, stirring halfway. Leave the oven on.
  3. In a large bowl, beat the coconut oil and honey together with a whisk. Add the eggs and beat well. Whisk in the mashed bananas and milk until blended. If the coconut oil solidifies, warm the mixture in 30-second intervals until it melts.
  4. Add the baking soda, vanilla, salt, and cinnamon and whisk to blend. Switch to a big spoon and stir in the flour just until combined; some lumps are okay. Gently fold in the toasted nuts.
  5. Pour the batter into the greased loaf pan and sprinkle the top lightly with additional cinnamon. For a swirled effect, run a knife tip across the batter in a zigzag.
  6. Bake until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean, 1 hour to 1 hour 5 minutes. Cool in the pan 10 minutes, then transfer to a rack for 20 minutes before slicing.
  7. This bread is moist, so it keeps just 2 to 3 days at room temperature, 5 to 7 days refrigerated, or up to 3 months frozen. Slice before freezing and thaw individual slices by toasting or microwaving.
