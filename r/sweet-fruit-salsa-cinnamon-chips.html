Sweet Fruit Salsa with Cinnamon Chips

Our Best Bites, p. 14

27 min · Serving size varies servings · 150 cal · 2g protein

Ingredients

  • FRUIT SALSA:
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1 teaspoon fresh lemon or lime juice
  • 1/8 teaspoon cinnamon, optional
  • 4 cups diced fruit (whatever is in season; use a variety of colors)
  • CINNAMON CHIPS:
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1/8 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 6 (6-inch) tortillas
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons butter, melted

Steps

  1. Fruit Salsa: Whisk together the honey and lime or lemon juice. Mix in the cinnamon, if desired.
  2. Fruit Salsa: Carefully combine the diced fruits. Toss gently with the honey mixture and serve immediately.
  3. Cinnamon Chips: Preheat oven to 350 degrees F.
  4. Cinnamon Chips: Combine sugar and cinnamon and set aside.
  5. Cinnamon Chips: Lightly brush one side of each tortilla with melted butter, then sprinkle with the cinnamon sugar.
  6. Cinnamon Chips: Use a pizza cutter to cut the tortillas into wedges. Place the wedges on an ungreased baking sheet and bake for about 10-12 minutes, or until they turn light golden and the edges curl up.
  7. Cinnamon Chips: Remove from oven and let cool. Serve alone or with the Fruit Salsa.
