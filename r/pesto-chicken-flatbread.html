Pesto Chicken Flatbread

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 57

20 min · Serves 6 servings · 690 cal · 41g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rotisserie chicken, chopped
  • 1 cup pesto, divided
  • 6 naan
  • 12 slices Mozzarella cheese
  • for topping: quartered grape tomatoes and balsamic glaze

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F.
  2. In a bowl, add the chicken and 1/2 cup of the pesto. Stir to coat.
  3. Layer the naan on a sheet pan. Spread the naan with the remaining 1/2 cup of pesto.
  4. Remove the Mozzarella from the package and soak up any extra moisture with a paper towel.
  5. Top the naan with the cheese slices and pesto chicken.
  6. Bake for 8-10 minutes.
  7. Top with grape tomatoes and balsamic glaze, if desired.
