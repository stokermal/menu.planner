Loaded Baked Potato Soup

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 155

40 min · Serves 6-8 servings · 470 cal · 12g protein

Note: so good!

Ingredients

  • 1 (1 qt) carton chicken stock
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 Tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 1/2 lbs Russet potatoes (about 3 large potatoes), peeled and diced
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded Cheddar cheese
  • TOPPINGS:
  • 8 slices fully cooked bacon
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1/2 cup shredded Cheddar cheese
  • Freshly ground black pepper

Steps

  1. Add the chicken stock, cream, and milk to the slow cooker. Whisk in the cornstarch.
  2. Add the onion and potatoes to the slow cooker. Season with the salt and pepper.
  3. Cook on low for 6 hours or on high for 4 hours, until the potatoes are fork tender.
  4. Using a potato masher, mash about half of the potatoes, leaving some in chunks. Stir in the sour cream and shredded cheese.
  5. Add the bacon to a paper towel-lined plate and microwave for 1-2 minutes, until crispy. Break the bacon into small pieces.
  6. Ladle the soup into bowls and top with the bacon, sour cream, shredded cheese, and pepper.
