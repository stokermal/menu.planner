Puffed French Toast

Our Best Bites, p. 64

35 min · Makes 12 triangles servings · 520 cal · 11g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 2 1/2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 12 slices Texas-style toast, cut in half diagonally
  • Canola oil, for frying (about 1/4 inch)
  • Cinnamon Sugar: 3/4 cup sugar plus 1 tablespoon plus 1 teaspoon cinnamon

Steps

  1. In a large skillet or frying pan, heat about 1/4 inch of canola oil over medium heat. If you have a candy thermometer, heat the oil to about 375 degrees F.
  2. If you're not using a thermometer, test the oil by flicking water into the pan: if it pops, reduce the heat; if hardly anything happens, turn up the heat; if it sizzles, it's just right.
  3. While the oil heats, whisk the eggs, sugar, salt, vanilla, milk, flour, and baking powder together in a shallow dish or pie plate.
  4. Working quickly, dip each half slice of bread in the batter on both sides (don't let it get too soaked). Gently shake off excess and place in the hot oil. Cook until puffed, golden brown, and crispy, about 3-5 minutes per side (babysit them). Remove and drain on a paper towel; keep cooked pieces warm in a low oven.
  5. When ready to serve, roll each piece in cinnamon sugar. If you're feeling wild, drizzle with maple syrup, Buttermilk Caramel Syrup (p.70), or Strawberry Sauce (p.71), or add a pat of Honey Butter (p.51).
