Berry Spinach Salad with Goat Cheese

House recipe

15 min · 6 as a side servings · 185 cal · 6g protein

Ingredients

  • 5 ounces baby spinach (about 8 cups)
  • 1 cup raspberries
  • 1 cup blueberries
  • 3/4 cup walnuts, toasted and roughly chopped
  • 4 ounces goat cheese, crumbled
  • Raspberry Vinaigrette, to serve

Steps

  1. Toast the walnuts in a dry skillet over medium heat for 3 to 5 minutes, shaking often, until they smell nutty. This is the single biggest upgrade in the whole salad. Cool before adding.
  2. Spread the spinach in a large shallow bowl.
  3. Scatter the raspberries and blueberries over the top. Add the raspberries last and do not stir — they break down fast.
  4. Add the toasted walnuts and crumbled goat cheese.
  5. Serve with the Raspberry Vinaigrette alongside and dress each plate.
