Banana-Coconut Pancakes

Love Real Food, p. 17

25 min · 8 or 9 medium pancakes; 2 large or 4 side servings servings · 300 cal · 7g protein

Note: Used apple cider syrup from Our Best Bites. Yum!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup white whole wheat flour or regular whole wheat flour
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • 3/4 cup light coconut milk
  • 1/3 cup mashed ripe banana (from about 1 medium-large banana)
  • 1 egg
  • Maple syrup or honey, plus more for serving (amount obscured in the photo)
  • 2 tablespoons coconut oil or unsalted butter, melted
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/4 cup unsweetened shredded coconut, for sprinkling
  • 1 medium banana, thinly sliced, for garnish

Steps

  1. If using an electric skillet, preheat to 350 degrees F. In a medium bowl, combine the flour, baking powder, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt. Whisk to blend.
  2. In a small bowl, combine the coconut milk, mashed banana, egg, maple syrup, coconut oil, and vanilla. Whisk until blended. Pour the liquid into the flour mixture and mix just until combined.
  3. If not using an electric skillet, heat a heavy cast iron skillet or nonstick griddle over medium-low. You are ready once a drop of water sizzles on contact. Lightly oil if needed.
  4. Using a 1/3-cup measure, scoop batter onto the hot skillet, leaving a couple inches around each pancake.
  5. Sprinkle shredded coconut over the raw tops while they cook. Cook until small bubbles form, 2 to 3 minutes. It is ready to flip when about 1/2 inch of the perimeter is matte instead of glossy. Flip and cook until lightly golden, 1 to 2 minutes more.
  6. Repeat with remaining batter, adding oil and adjusting heat as needed. Serve immediately or keep warm in a 200 degree F oven. Top with sliced bananas and serve with more maple syrup or honey.
  7. Leftovers keep in the refrigerator up to 3 days or frozen up to 2 months. To reheat, stack and wrap in a paper towel before gently microwaving.
