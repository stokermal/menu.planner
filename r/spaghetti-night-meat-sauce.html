Spaghetti Night with Meat Sauce

House recipe

25 min · about 8 servings · 540 cal · 36g protein

Note: Luke's favorite.

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds 90/10 ground beef
  • 2 jars (24 ounces each) Rao's marinara sauce
  • 1 pound Barilla Protein+ spaghetti
  • Salt for the pasta water
  • Grated Parmesan, to serve

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil.
  2. Brown the ground beef in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up as it goes. At 90/10 there is usually little fat to drain.
  3. If anyone at the table is vegetarian, set aside a cup of plain marinara now, before the sauce meets the meat.
  4. Add the marinara to the beef, lower the heat, and simmer 10 minutes while the pasta cooks. It only needs to heat through and marry — Rao's is already a finished sauce.
  5. Cook the spaghetti to al dente, 10 to 11 minutes. Protein+ holds its bite well, but it turns soft fast once it goes past done, so start tasting at 9 minutes.
  6. Reserve a cup of pasta water before draining. A splash loosens the sauce and helps it cling.
  7. Serve the sauce over the spaghetti with Parmesan at the table.
