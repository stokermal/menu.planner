Jalapeno-Lime Dressing

Love Real Food, p. 53

5 min · about 1 cup servings · 110 cal · 0g protein

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 cup lime juice (from about 4 limes)
  • 1 small jalapeno, seeded, deribbed, and roughly chopped
  • 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 clove garlic, roughly chopped
  • 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • Pinch of red pepper flakes (optional)

Steps

  1. In a blender, combine all of the ingredients except the pepper flakes and blend until smooth.
  2. Taste and blend in additional salt if the dressing needs a little more oomph. If you'd like a spicier dressing, blend in the pepper flakes.
  3. If the dressing is too bold for your liking, dilute it with a little more olive oil and blend again.
