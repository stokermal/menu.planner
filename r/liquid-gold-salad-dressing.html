Liquid Gold Salad Dressing

Love Real Food, p. 52

5 min · about 1 cup servings · 100 cal · 0g protein

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/3 cup lemon juice (from about 2 lemons)
  • 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
  • 2 tablespoons honey or maple syrup
  • 3 cloves garlic, pressed or minced
  • 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • Generous amount of freshly ground black pepper
  • Pinch of red pepper flakes (optional)

Steps

  1. Combine all of the ingredients and whisk to blend.
  2. The dressing should be pleasantly zippy. If it is too tart, whisk in a little more olive oil until it suits your taste buds. Taste and add additional salt and/or pepper if it doesn't knock your socks off.
