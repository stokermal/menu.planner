Pico de Gallo

Our Best Bites, p. 48

15 min · Makes about 1 1/2 cups servings · 12 cal · 0g protein

Ingredients

  • 3 medium Roma tomatoes
  • 1/2 small onion (white is traditional; red adds color and flavor)
  • 1 smallish jalapeno pepper
  • Juice of 1/2-1 lime, to taste
  • Approximately 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
  • Kosher salt, to taste

Steps

  1. Cut the ends off the tomatoes and slice in half lengthwise. Under running water, gently run your finger under the membrane to remove the seeds (don't worry if a few make it in). Cut the tomatoes into small chunks and place in a small mixing bowl.
  2. Finely dice the onion and toss with the tomatoes.
  3. Finely chop the jalapeno, removing the seeds if desired (the seeds hold most of the heat). Toss with the tomatoes and onions.
  4. Add the fresh-squeezed juice of half of the lime, stir, and taste. Add more if desired.
  5. Add the cilantro and mix well. Add a generous sprinkling of kosher salt, taste, and add more if desired. Refrigerate for 30 minutes if possible.
