Spicy Honey Chicken

Our Best Bites, p. 119

20 min · 4-6 servings · 410 cal · 36g protein

Ingredients

  • 8 boneless, skinless chicken thighs (about 2 pounds); thighs work better than breasts here
  • 2 teaspoons vegetable oil
  • Glaze: 1/2 cup honey
  • Glaze: 1 tablespoon cider vinegar
  • Rub: 2 teaspoons granulated garlic
  • Rub: 2 teaspoons chili powder
  • Rub: 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • Rub: 1/2 teaspoon coriander
  • Rub: 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • Rub: 1 teaspoon cumin
  • Rub: 1/2 teaspoon chipotle chili powder

Steps

  1. Preheat grill to medium-high heat, about 400 degrees F.
  2. Combine rub ingredients in a small bowl.
  3. Trim visible fat off the chicken thighs, rinse, and pat dry. Drizzle oil over chicken and rub it in with your hands. Rub the spice rub into the thighs. Grill 3-5 minutes per side.
  4. For the glaze: while chicken cooks, warm honey in the microwave about 15 seconds. Whisk in vinegar. Reserve 2 tablespoons of glaze and brush the rest on the chicken in the final moments of cooking. Remove from grill and let stand 3-5 minutes. Drizzle the reserved 2 tablespoons over the chicken while it stands.
