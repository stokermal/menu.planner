Berry Protein Smoothie

House recipe

5 min · about 6 cups, 6 servings servings · 250 cal · 20g protein

Ingredients

  • 4 cups frozen mixed berries
  • 2 bananas
  • 2 scoops vanilla protein powder
  • 1 1/2 cups plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 1/2 cups milk
  • Honey to taste, only if needed

Steps

  1. Liquid first: milk, then yogurt, then the bananas and protein powder, with the frozen berries on top.
  2. Blend on high 45 to 60 seconds until smooth. Berry seeds need the extra time — a weaker blender leaves them gritty, which is where a high-powered one earns its keep.
  3. Taste before sweetening. Frozen berries are more tart than mango, so this one occasionally does want a spoonful of honey.
  4. Blueberries and blackberries will turn it a muddy purple-grey. It tastes the same; if the colour bothers anyone, lean on strawberries and raspberries instead.
