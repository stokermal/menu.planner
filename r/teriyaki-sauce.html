Teriyaki Sauce

Our Best Bites, p. 46

10 min · Makes 1 1/2 cups servings · 21 cal · 0g protein

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup cider vinegar
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 1 tablespoon cold water

Steps

  1. Combine the sugar, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, ginger, and black pepper in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat.
  2. While the soy sauce mixture is heating, whisk together the cornstarch and cold water. When the soy sauce mixture comes to a full boil, whisk in the cornstarch mixture and stir the sauce until thickened and clear. Remove from heat and allow to cool.
