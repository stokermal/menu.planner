Spicy Breakfast Fajitas with Fried Eggs and Guacamole

Love Real Food, p. 32

35 min · 6 fajitas; plan on 2 to 3 per person servings · 555 cal · 20g protein

Ingredients

  • Veggies: 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • Veggies: 2 red, yellow, or orange bell peppers, sliced into 1/2-inch-wide strips
  • Veggies: 1/2 medium white onion, sliced into 1/2-inch-wide wedges
  • Veggies: 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • Veggies: 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
  • Veggies: pinch of red pepper flakes
  • Veggies: fine sea salt
  • Veggies: 2 cloves garlic, pressed or minced
  • Veggies: 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 6 corn tortillas or small flour tortillas
  • 6 teaspoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
  • 6 eggs
  • Quick Guacamole (page 34)
  • 1/3 cup crumbled feta cheese (about 2 ounces)
  • Handful of fresh cilantro, chopped
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Hot sauce and/or your favorite salsa

Steps

  1. To prepare the veggies: heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium. Once shimmering, add the bell peppers, onion, cumin, chili powder, red pepper flakes, and a few dashes of salt. Toss, cover, and cook, stirring occasionally, until easily pierced by a fork, 5 to 8 minutes. Uncover and keep cooking, stirring often, until lightly charred on the edges, about 5 minutes more. Add the garlic, toss, and cook another minute. Off the heat, drizzle in the lime juice, toss, season with salt, and cover to keep warm.
  2. To prepare the fajitas: in a small skillet over medium heat, warm each tortilla on both sides. Stack the warmed tortillas on a plate and cover with a kitchen towel.
  3. In the same skillet, heat 1 teaspoon of oil over medium until shimmering. Crack an egg into the skillet without breaking the yolk. Fry, lifting and tilting the pan to redistribute the oil, until the whites are set and the yolk is done to your liking. Transfer to a plate and repeat with the remaining eggs.
  4. To assemble the breakfast fajitas, place a fried egg on each tortilla, followed by a generous spread of guacamole down the length of the egg white. Top with some veggies. Sprinkle with feta, cilantro, and freshly ground black pepper. Serve with hot sauce and/or salsa on the side.
