Pesto Tortelloni with Tomatoes and Mozzarella

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 75

15 min · Serves 8 servings · 480 cal · 19g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 (24 oz) package five cheese tortelloni
  • 1 cup pesto
  • 1 cup grape tomatoes, halved
  • 1 cup Mozzarella balls
  • for garnish: fresh basil and shredded Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Boil the tortelloni according to the package directions.
  2. Drain the pasta and add the pesto, grape tomatoes, and Mozzarella balls.
  3. Serve warm or cold.
  4. Garnish with basil and Parmesan, if desired.
