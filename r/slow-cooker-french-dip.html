Slow Cooker French Dip Sandwiches

Our Best Bites, p. 134

15 min · approximately 12-14 servings · 520 cal · 37g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 (2 1/2-3 pounds) beef roast
  • Kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 (1-ounce) packages dry onion soup mix
  • 2 cups water
  • 2 (14.5-ounce) cans beef broth
  • 1 (16-ounce) package sliced Swiss cheese
  • Crusty buns or rolls (or Hoagies on p. 32)

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pot or skillet over medium-high heat. While the oil heats, sprinkle all sides of the roast with kosher salt and freshly ground pepper.
  2. When the oil is very hot, carefully place the roast in the pan and sear on all sides. This should take only a few minutes; you just want to quickly brown the roast.
  3. Transfer the roast to your slow cooker. Sprinkle onion soup mix over the roast and add water and beef broth.
  4. Cook on high 4-6 hours or on low 8-10 hours. You can also cook on high until it begins to boil and then switch to low; the book prefers this method for tenderness.
  5. When the meat shreds easily with a fork, shred the entire roast. Serve on sliced crusty rolls with a slice of Swiss cheese. Optionally broil to toast the bun and melt the cheese. Serve with the juices from the slow cooker as au jus for dipping.
