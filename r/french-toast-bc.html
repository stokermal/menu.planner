French Toast

Betty Crocker Cookbook, p. 79

20 min · 8 slices servings · 135 cal · 5g protein

Ingredients

  • 3 large eggs
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1/8 teaspoon salt
  • 8 slices sandwich bread or 1-inch-thick slices French bread

Steps

  1. In a medium bowl, beat the eggs, milk, sugar, vanilla, and salt with a wire whisk or hand beater until well mixed. Pour into a shallow bowl.
  2. Heat a griddle or skillet over medium heat, or to 375 degrees F. To test the griddle, sprinkle a few drops of water on it; if the water bubbles jump around, the heat is right. Grease the griddle with vegetable oil if necessary, or spray with cooking spray before heating.
  3. Dip the bread into the egg mixture, then place on the griddle. Cook about 4 minutes on each side, or until golden brown.
