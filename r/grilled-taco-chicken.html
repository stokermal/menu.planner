Grilled Taco Chicken

Our Best Bites, p. 114

24 min · 4 servings · 195 cal · 36g protein

Note: YUM! (Checkmark next to the serving suggestion.)

Ingredients

  • 1 pound boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 2-3 juicy limes
  • 1-2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced or pressed
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

Steps

  1. Place chicken breasts in a heavy-duty zip-top plastic bag.
  2. Squeeze limes over chicken. Add a splash of vinegar and the minced garlic. Seal the bag and shake to distribute the marinade. Marinate 4-8 hours.
  3. Preheat grill to medium-high heat (about 400 degrees F).
  4. Combine chili powder, cumin, salt, and pepper in a small bowl. Remove chicken from bag and discard the used marinade. Rub the spices into the chicken.
  5. Grill 7 minutes, turn, and grill 5-7 more minutes (5 for smaller breasts, no longer than 7 for larger). Remove to a clean plate and let stand 5 minutes before slicing.
