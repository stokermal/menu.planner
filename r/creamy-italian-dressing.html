Creamy Italian Dressing

House recipe

5 min · about 1 1/2 cups servings · 95 cal · 0g protein

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/3 cup white wine or red wine vinegar
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tablespoon Italian seasoning
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

Steps

  1. Whisk everything together in a jar or blend until smooth.
  2. Chill at least 30 minutes before serving. The Italian seasoning needs time to soften and bloom, and the dressing thickens as it sits.
  3. Shake before each use.
