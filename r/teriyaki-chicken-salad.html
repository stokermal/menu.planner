Teriyaki Chicken Salad

Our Best Bites, p. 83

40 min · Serves 4-6 servings · 290 cal · 24g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1 1/2 cups Kikkoman Teriyaki Baste and Glaze or 1 recipe Teriyaki Sauce (p. 46)
  • 2 stalks celery, finely chopped
  • 1/3 cup sliced green onions, chopped
  • 1/3 to 1/2 cup light mayonnaise
  • 1 mango, cut into small cubes (or 1 [6-ounce] can of Mandarin oranges, drained)
  • 1 (8-ounce) can pineapple tidbits or roughly chopped chunks
  • 1/4 cup sliced almonds, toasted
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Croissants or pitas
  • Lettuce leaves

Steps

  1. Reserving about 2/3 cup of Teriyaki Sauce, marinate the chicken in the remaining sauce for at least 4 hours. Grill the chicken on medium heat for about 7 minutes per side, basting regularly with some of the reserved sauce.
  2. While the chicken is grilling, combine the celery and green onions in a small mixing bowl or a plastic storage container with a lid.
  3. When the chicken is done, allow it to stand for about 10 minutes and then cut into bite-sized pieces. Toss with the celery and onions, then add mayonnaise and mix thoroughly. Start with 1/3 cup and go from there. Add 2-3 (or more) tablespoons of Teriyaki Sauce to taste. You want some of the teriyaki flavor without it being overwhelming. Refrigerate for at least 4-6 hours.
  4. Just before serving, add the mango (or oranges), pineapple, and nuts. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve with lettuce in a croissant or pita, or on a leaf of lettuce.
