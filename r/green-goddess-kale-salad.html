Green Goddess Kale Salad

Love Real Food, p. 67

25 min · 2 large salads or 4 generous side salads servings · 500 cal · 14g protein

Ingredients

  • Salad: 1 medium bunch curly kale (about 8 ounces), ribs removed and leaves chopped
  • Salad: fine sea salt
  • Salad: 1 cup seeded and chopped cucumber (about 1/2 medium)
  • Salad: 1 cup finely chopped carrots (about 3)
  • Salad: 1/2 cup finely chopped radishes (about 4)
  • Salad: 1/3 cup raw sunflower seeds
  • Salad: 1/4 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • Dressing: 3 tablespoons lemon juice (from 1 to 1 1/2 lemons), or more if needed
  • Dressing: 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • Dressing: 2 tablespoons water
  • Dressing: 1 avocado, diced
  • Dressing: 1/3 cup lightly packed fresh flat-leaf parsley
  • Dressing: 2 tablespoons chopped green onions (1 to 2)
  • Dressing: 1 tablespoon fresh tarragon leaves or roughly chopped fresh basil or cilantro
  • Dressing: 1 clove garlic, roughly chopped
  • Dressing: 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • Dressing: freshly ground black pepper

Steps

  1. To prepare the salad: transfer the chopped kale to a big salad bowl. Sprinkle a small pinch of salt over it and massage the leaves by scrunching big handfuls at a time, until the leaves darken, become fragrant, and reduce in volume by about a third. Add the cucumber, carrots, and radishes.
  2. In a small skillet, combine the sunflower seeds, olive oil, and a few dashes of salt. Toast over medium, stirring frequently, until fragrant and lightly golden on the edges, 4 to 5 minutes. Pour into the salad bowl.
  3. To make the dressing: in a food processor or blender, combine the lemon juice, olive oil, water, avocado, parsley, green onions, tarragon, garlic, salt, and several twists of pepper. Blend, starting low and increasing to medium, until smooth and creamy, scraping down the sides as needed. It should be pretty zippy, so taste and blend in more lemon juice if needed.
  4. When ready to eat, drizzle the dressing over the salad. Kale salads take more dressing than other salads. Toss until lightly coated and serve immediately. Keeps well chilled up to 2 days.
