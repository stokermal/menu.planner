Barbecue Chicken Cobb Salad

Our Best Bites, p. 77

20 min · 4 servings · 750 cal · 45g protein

Note: Bracket drawn around the blue cheese dressing line.

Ingredients

  • 12 cups Romaine lettuce
  • 8-12 ounces grilled barbecue chicken
  • 8 ounces bacon, cooked and crumbled
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, seeded and diced
  • 1 large avocado, pitted and diced
  • 3 hard-boiled eggs, peeled and diced
  • 1/3 cup sliced green onions
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • Buttermilk Ranch Dressing (p. 84) with a few spoonfuls of barbecue sauce stirred in (household swap - no blue cheese)

Steps

  1. Divide the lettuce among 4 plates.
  2. Top with chopped grilled barbecue chicken, bacon, tomatoes, avocado, hard-boiled egg, green onions, cheese, and blue cheese dressing. Serve immediately.
