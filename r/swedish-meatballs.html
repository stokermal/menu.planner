Swedish Meatballs

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 147

25 min · Serves 6 servings · 560 cal · 20g protein

Ingredients

  • 30 frozen Italian-style beef meatballs
  • 1 1/2 cups water
  • 2 tsp Better Than Bouillon Beef Base
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 2 Tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp Italian Spice & Salt (or 1/2 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning)
  • 1/4 tsp pepper
  • Pinch of nutmeg
  • 1 (17.6 oz) bag pasta

Steps

  1. Add the meatballs to the slow cooker.
  2. In a mixing bowl, whisk together the water, beef base, heavy cream, cornstarch, Worcestershire sauce, Italian Spice & Salt, pepper, and nutmeg. Pour over the meatballs.
  3. Cook on low for 5 hours or on high for 3 hours.
  4. Boil the pasta according to the package directions.
  5. Serve the meatballs and sauce over the pasta.
