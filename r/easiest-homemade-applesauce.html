The Easiest Homemade Applesauce—Ever!

100 Days of Real Food: Fast & Fabulous, p. 147

192 min · Makes 3 1/2 to 4 cups servings · 95 cal · 0g protein

Ingredients

  • 3 pounds apples, any variety or mix will do
  • 1 teaspoon fresh lemon juice
  • Ground cinnamon, for serving (optional)

Steps

  1. Use the apple slicer/corer tool (if you have one) to cut the apples. I like to leave the peels on, but you can remove them if you prefer. If you don't have a slicer/corer, cut the apples into 8 (or 10) equal wedges. Cut each apple wedge in thirds to make 1-inch pieces.
  2. Toss together the apples, lemon juice, and 1 tablespoon water in the slow cooker. Cover and cook on high for 3 hours, or until the apple chunks are so soft they break apart when you pierce them with a fork.
  3. Mash the apples with a potato masher (or the back of a fork). If you left the peels on and really want to smooth things out, you can always use an immersion blender as well.
  4. Serve warm with a little cinnamon (if using) sprinkled on top or store in the fridge or freezer for another day.
