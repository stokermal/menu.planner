BBQ Chicken Salad

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 53

22 min · Serves 6-8 servings · 560 cal · 28g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rotisserie chicken, chopped
  • 1 cup BBQ sauce
  • 6 slices fully cooked bacon, chopped
  • 6-8 cups spring salad mix
  • 1 cup corn (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 (15 oz) can black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1/2 red onion, diced
  • 1 cup shredded Mexican cheese
  • 6 hard-boiled eggs, diced
  • 1 cup grape tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 cup ranch dressing

Steps

  1. In a bowl, add the chicken and BBQ sauce. Stir to coat.
  2. Add the bacon to a paper towel-lined plate and microwave for 1-2 minutes, until crispy.
  3. In a large serving bowl, add the spring salad mix.
  4. Layer on the remaining ingredients. Drizzle with ranch dressing, or serve it on the side.
