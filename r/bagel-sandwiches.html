Bagel Sandwiches

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 25

20 min · Serves 6 servings · 560 cal · 30g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbsp salted butter
  • 6 eggs
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • 6 bagels
  • 12 slices fully cooked bacon
  • 6 slices Cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup spinach
  • for serving: hot sauce or salsa

Steps

  1. Heat a nonstick skillet over medium heat and add the butter.
  2. Crack the eggs into the skillet. Salt and pepper the eggs, to taste, and cook for about 3 minutes, until the egg whites are set. Flip the eggs and cook for about 1 more minute.
  3. Cut the bagels in half and toast.
  4. Add the bacon to a paper towel-lined plate and microwave for 1-2 minutes, until crispy.
  5. Add one egg, a slice of cheese, 2 pieces of bacon, and a small handful of spinach to each bagel.
  6. Serve with hot sauce or salsa, if desired.
