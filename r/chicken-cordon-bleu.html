Chicken Cordon Bleu

Our Best Bites, p. 112

60 min · 6 servings · 360 cal · 34g protein

Note: YUM! Would try w/ just Panko too.

Ingredients

  • 3 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon seasoned salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella, provolone, or Swiss cheese
  • 6-12 slices thinly sliced ham
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 1 cup panko bread crumbs
  • 1/2 cup freshly shredded Parmesan cheese
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons butter, melted

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F. Spray a baking sheet with nonstick cooking spray.
  2. Place a breast on a cutting board with your non-dominant hand on top. Cut each breast completely in half horizontally. Place each piece between two pieces of plastic wrap and pound with a meat mallet to about 1/4 to 1/2 inch thick. Repeat; you should have 6 pieces of chicken.
  3. Combine seasonings and sprinkle evenly over one side of each piece. Divide shredded cheese among all pieces, layering on top of the spices. On top of the cheese, layer 1-2 pieces of ham. Roll each piece up and secure with toothpicks.
  4. Place flour in a shallow bowl, eggs in another, and panko plus Parmesan in a third, stirring to combine.
  5. Working with one piece at a time, dredge in flour, then eggs, then bread crumb mixture. Press the crumb mixture onto all sides.
  6. Place chicken on the prepared baking sheet. Drizzle melted butter evenly over chicken. Bake 25-35 minutes or until internal temperature reaches 165 degrees F. If desired, broil briefly to brown the tops.
  7. Cool 10 minutes before serving. Remove toothpicks.
