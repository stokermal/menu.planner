Outrageous Herbaceous Chickpea Salad

Love Real Food, p. 71

15 min · 4 side salads servings · 280 cal · 9g protein

Note: Better next day.

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (15 ounces each) chickpeas, rinsed and drained, or 3 cups cooked chickpeas
  • 1 medium red bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 1/2 cups chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley (about 1 bunch)
  • 1/2 cup chopped red onion (about 1/2 small)
  • 1/2 cup chopped celery (about 2 ribs)
  • 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 tablespoons lemon juice (from 1 to 1 1/2 lemons), or more if needed
  • 2 cloves garlic, pressed or minced
  • 1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper

Steps

  1. In a medium bowl, combine the chickpeas, bell pepper, parsley, onion, celery, olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, salt, and pepper to taste. Toss until combined. Taste and add additional lemon juice, salt, or pepper if needed.
  2. Serve immediately, or chill until ready to serve. Leftovers keep well chilled up to 4 days.
