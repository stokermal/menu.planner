Pizza Sauce

Our Best Bites, p. 155

5 min · about 1 1/2 cups; covers two average-sized pizzas servings · 22 cal · 1g protein

Note: Checkmarked in the book.

Ingredients

  • 1 (6-ounce) can tomato paste
  • 6 ounces water (just use the empty tomato paste can)
  • 3 tablespoons Garlic Bread Seasoning (p. 53)
  • 1 tablespoon sugar or honey
  • 3/4 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes, optional

Steps

  1. Empty the tomato paste into a bowl and add the water a few tablespoons at a time, stirring constantly until combined.
  2. Add the remaining ingredients, stir to combine, and allow to stand until ready to use. Covers two average-sized pizzas.
