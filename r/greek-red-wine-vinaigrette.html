Greek Red Wine Vinaigrette

House recipe

5 min · about 1 cup servings · 85 cal · 0g protein

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 1/4 cup red wine vinegar
  • Juice of 1/2 lemon
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

Steps

  1. Shake everything together in a jar until emulsified. The Dijon is what holds the oil and vinegar together, so do not skip it.
  2. Crush the dried oregano between your fingers as you add it to wake it up.
  3. Keeps a week in the fridge. Let it sit out a few minutes and shake again before serving, since olive oil thickens when cold.
