Teriyaki Meatball Bowls

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 71

25 min · Serves 6-8 servings · 520 cal · 24g protein

Note: YUM!

Ingredients

  • 30 Aidell's Chicken Meatballs (contents of 1 bag)
  • 1 cup teriyaki sauce
  • 1 1/2 lb broccoli (about 1/2 of a fresh bag)
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • 6 (7.4 oz) microwavable rice bowls
  • for garnish: sesame seeds and chopped green onions

Steps

  1. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat and add the meatballs. Cook for 6-8 minutes, until warmed through. Add the teriyaki sauce to coat.
  2. Steam the broccoli for 5-6 minutes in a steamer basket. Season with salt and pepper, to taste.
  3. Microwave the rice according to the package directions.
  4. Add the rice to a bowl and top with the meatballs and broccoli.
  5. Garnish with sesame seeds and green onions, if desired.
