Chicken Tikka Masala

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 91

15 min · Serves 6-8 servings · 650 cal · 30g protein

Note: Would be better regular rice

Ingredients

  • 2 pouches Sukhi's Chicken Tikka Masala
  • 1 (13.66 oz) can coconut milk
  • 1 (15 oz) can garbanzo beans, drained and rinsed
  • 6 (7.4 oz) microwavable rice bowls
  • 6-8 naan
  • for garnish: cilantro

Steps

  1. Empty the chicken tikka masala into a large saucepan over medium heat. Add the coconut milk and garbanzo beans. Stir and cook until heated through.
  2. Microwave the rice according to the package directions.
  3. Heat the naan in the microwave for about 30 seconds under a damp paper towel.
  4. Serve the tikka masala over rice with naan on the side.
  5. Garnish with cilantro, if desired.
