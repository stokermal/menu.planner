Strawberry Sauce

Our Best Bites, p. 71

10 min · Makes about 2 cups servings · 30 cal · 0g protein

Ingredients

  • 1 pint strawberries, washed, hulled, and roughly chopped
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon almond extract or vanilla

Steps

  1. Combine berries, sugar, and vanilla in a medium saucepan and bring to a simmer over medium heat. Cook for five minutes, stirring constantly and breaking the strawberries up with a wooden spoon.
  2. After five minutes, remove from heat and allow to cool before blending. When cool enough, transfer to a blender and pulse until desired consistency is reached. Refrigerate unused sauce up to 1 week or freeze up to 3 months.
