Creamed Eggs

Costco One Stop Meals, p. 21

25 min · Serves 6-8 servings · 430 cal · 19g protein

Ingredients

  • 4 Tbsp salted butter
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 3 cups milk
  • 12 hard-boiled eggs
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • 1 loaf sliced bread
  • for garnish: chives

Steps

  1. Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat.
  2. Whisk in the flour and stir for about 1 minute.
  3. Slowly pour in the milk. Bring to a gentle boil, stirring frequently. Reduce the heat to simmer, until the sauce begins to thicken.
  4. Meanwhile, grate the hard-boiled eggs.
  5. Add the eggs to the milk sauce and season with salt and pepper, to taste.
  6. Toast the bread and spread with butter. Top with the creamed eggs.
  7. Garnish with chives, if desired.
