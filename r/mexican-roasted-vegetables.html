Mexican Roasted Vegetables

House recipe

50 min · about 6 as a side, or enough to top 8 burrito bowls servings · 210 cal · 3g protein

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds sweet potatoes, peeled and diced into 1/2-inch cubes
  • 2 bell peppers, seeded and cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 large red onion, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 3 tablespoons olive or avocado oil, divided
  • 2 teaspoons chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • Optional to finish: juice of 1/2 lime, chopped cilantro

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425 degrees F.
  2. Dicing the sweet potatoes small is what makes the staging work — 1/2-inch cubes roast in about half the time a wedge would.
  3. Toss the diced sweet potatoes with 2 tablespoons of the oil and half the chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Spread on a sheet pan in a single layer, leaving space between the pieces so they roast rather than steam.
  4. Roast the sweet potatoes alone for 15 minutes.
  5. While they roast, toss the peppers and onion with the remaining 1 tablespoon oil and the rest of the seasoning.
  6. Pull the pan out, push the sweet potatoes to one side, add the peppers and onion, and stir everything together. Return to the oven.
  7. Roast another 15 to 20 minutes, until the sweet potatoes are tender and the peppers and onions have charred edges. Stir once partway through.
  8. Finish with lime juice and cilantro if using. Serve hot.
